## The spiders we treat in Jupiter

The vast majority of spiders in Jupiter are harmless and actually help by eating other pests — but two are worth taking seriously: the **black widow** and the increasingly common **brown widow**. Both tuck into exactly the places people reach without looking: garages, meter and irrigation boxes, patio furniture, pool cages, and grill covers.

Spiders are also a symptom — they gather where there are insects to eat. So the most effective spider control does two things at once: knock down the spiders and webs directly, and reduce the insect prey drawing them in.

## What we watch for

### Black Widows

Glossy black with the red hourglass; hide in dark, undisturbed spots. A bite is a genuine medical concern — we treat their harborage directly.

### Brown Widows

Now very common across South Florida; spiky egg sacs under rails, furniture, and boxes. Less venomous but everywhere — we knock them down and treat.

### Nuisance Web Spiders

Orb-weavers and house spiders that string webs across eaves, entries, and screens — cleared and kept down.
