## Why Victoria Park homes need year-round coverage

Victoria Park is one of Fort Lauderdale’s oldest, most walkable neighborhoods — brick streets, a dense oak-and-palm canopy, and character homes just off downtown and the Intracoastal. That canopy is a highway for roof rats, and decades-old construction develops the gaps ants, roaches, and rodents use to get inside.

Nearby canals keep mosquitoes and no-see-ums active, and subterranean termites work the warm soil under older slabs. We seal entry points and treat the source while respecting the character of an older home.

## The pests Victoria Park homes face

### Roof Rats & Rodents

The mature oak-and-palm canopy puts roof rats onto rooftops and into attics of older homes.

### Drywood & Subterranean Termites

Older wood framing attracts drywood termites, while subterranean termites attack from the soil.

### Ghost Ants & Palmetto Bugs

Older slabs and moist landscaping feed ghost ants; American roaches push in from drains after rain.

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

Nearby canals and the Intracoastal breed biting midges and mosquitoes that drift into yards at dusk.
