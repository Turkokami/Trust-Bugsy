## **Why Are Mosquitoes in Your House?**

![Illustration showing common entry points for mosquitoes in a house, including windows, doors, and vents.](https://trustbugsy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Common-Entry-Points-for-Mosquitoes-in-a-House-1.webp)

Mosquitoes are usually drawn indoors by light, standing water, and the scent of carbon dioxide (yes, from breathing). Once inside, they hide in dark, humid places like laundry rooms, bathrooms, or behind furniture. South Florida’s subtropical conditions create a perfect storm: frequent rains, dense vegetation, and warm temperatures mean mosquitoes have no shortage of breeding grounds.

## **Natural Home Remedies to Kill Mosquitoes Inside**

If you’re not ready to call in the pros yet, try these tried-and-true home remedies that many South Florida homeowners swear by:

### **Coffee Grounds in Standing Water**

- Mosquitoes lay their eggs in stagnant water, but sprinkling coffee grounds into these areas can stop the larvae from developing. This method is safe, eco-friendly, and uses something you probably already have at home.

### **Camphor or Essential Oils**

- Camphor tablets or oil diffusers using eucalyptus, citronella, or peppermint oil can help repel mosquitoes. These natural options not only smell good but also make your home a lot less inviting for mosquitoes.

### **DIY Mosquito Traps**

- Cut a bottle in half and invert the top into the base. Fill the bottom with sugar water and yeast. Mosquitoes are attracted to the carbon dioxide the mixture produces—and get trapped inside.

### **Lemon and Clove Trick**

- Cut a lemon in half, stick in some cloves, and place them in corners or near windows. The scent is a natural deterrent mosquitoes don’t like.

## **FAQs About Getting Rid of Mosquitoes Indoors**

### Do citronella candles really work indoors?
A: They help in small, enclosed spaces—but they’re not a long-term solution. For real relief, consider essential oil diffusers or integrated pest management.

### How do I find where mosquitoes are hiding in my home?
A: Check damp areas like bathrooms, laundry rooms, closets, and under sinks. They also hide in potted plants or anywhere water collects.

### How often should I treat my home for mosquitoes?
A: In South Florida, monthly or bi-monthly mosquito treatments are recommended during peak seasons (April through October).

### Is your mosquito treatment safe for pets and children?
A: Absolutely. Bugsy’s uses eco-conscious, family-friendly products that are tough on pests but gentle on people and pets.

### Do indoor plants attract mosquitoes?
A: Yes—especially if they hold water in trays or if the soil stays overly moist. Letting the topsoil dry out between waterings helps.
