# Fruit Flies

Pest Library

## Fruit Flies

Drosophila species

Tiny tan flies with red eyes that seem to appear from nowhere around ripe fruit, drains, and recycling — because they breed in the thin film of fermenting residue those spots hold.

## How to identify them

Fruit flies are small — about an eighth of an inch — tan to brownish, and many have distinctive red eyes. They hover and dart around fruit bowls, sinks, trash, and recycling, and their numbers can build with surprising speed.

## Behavior and biology in South Florida

Fruit flies breed in moist, fermenting, sugary material: overripe produce, spilled juice or soda, the residue in recycling bins and trash cans, mop buckets, and the organic film inside drains. A female lays hundreds of eggs, and the entire cycle takes only about a week to ten days, so a single forgotten banana or dirty drain can seed a persistent problem.

Because the breeding sites are often hidden — a drain, the gap under an appliance, a recycling bin — the flies seem to reappear no matter how many are swatted.

## Signs of an infestation

- Small flies hovering around fruit, sinks, trash, and recycling
- Activity concentrated near drains and under or behind appliances
- Numbers that rebound quickly after cleaning the obvious fruit
- Flies in bars, break rooms, and kitchens where sugary residue collects

## Why they are a problem

Fruit flies move between decaying material and food surfaces and can carry bacteria and yeasts, making them a contamination concern in kitchens and food-service settings. They are also a stubborn nuisance, since killing adults does nothing about the eggs and larvae in the breeding source.

## How Bugsy’s treats fruit flies

Elimination means finding and removing the breeding sources. Because a missed source restarts the cycle, we focus on the source, not just the swarm.

## Preventing fruit flies

- Refrigerate or discard overripe and damaged produce
- Rinse recyclables and empty trash and recycling regularly
- Clean drains and remove the organic film where larvae breed
- Wipe up sugary and alcoholic spills promptly
- Keep mop buckets and cleaning tools dry between uses
