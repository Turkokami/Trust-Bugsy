## Why waterfront living means different pests

Boynton’s Intracoastal and beachside homes sit against seawalls, docks, and the inlet, with water on the doorstep. It’s prized living — and it reshapes which pests you fight.

Seawalls, docks, and dense waterfront landscaping give roof rats continuous elevated cover to the roofline. The Intracoastal and inlet breed mosquitoes and, especially, **no-see-ums** that come off the water at dawn and dusk onto docks and balconies. Standard inland routes miss what a waterfront home needs.
