Problem with Basic Pest Control

**We Go Beyond the Basics to Protect Your Home**

Many pest control companies in Florida stop at a simple spray around your home’s foundation. That might help for a little while — but it leaves the real problem areas untouched.

**What Others Miss:**

- Cobwebs and egg sacs in eaves and soffits
- Pest breeding grounds around windows and doors
- Vulnerable areas like garages and lanais
- Gaps that need weatherstripping
- Entry points for palmetto bugs and other invaders

The result? Temporary relief that doesn’t address the root cause.

**Our Comprehensive Protection Process**
