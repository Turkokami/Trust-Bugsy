## Why Lake Worth Beach attics need the right insulation

In Lake Worth Beach’s climate, your attic insulation works hardest of anything in the house — holding cool air in through months of heat and humidity. When rodents contaminate and mat it down, or when it’s simply old and thin, it stops doing its job and your cooling bills climb.

When we replace it, we recommend **TAP** — Thermal, Acoustical, Pest-control insulation. It’s a borate-treated product that insulates and dampens sound better than standard insulation while actively resisting insects, so the attic works better and stays less inviting to pests.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is TAP insulation?
TAP stands for Thermal, Acoustical, Pest-control insulation — a borate-treated cellulose product that insulates and dampens sound while resisting insects like roaches, ants, and silverfish.

### Will new insulation really lower my cooling bills?
Proper coverage and R-value make a real difference in Lake Worth Beach’s climate, where cooling runs much of the year. Damaged or thin insulation lets that conditioned air escape.

### Should I replace insulation after rodents?
Yes — rodent-contaminated, matted insulation is both a health issue and an energy loss. Replacing it with pest-resistant TAP is the natural finish to a rodent job.

### Is TAP safe for my family and pets?
Yes. The borate treatment targets insects and is applied within the attic; your technician will explain the product and process.

### Do you handle the cleanup and the insulation together?
Yes. We coordinate rodent exclusion, attic sanitation, and insulation replacement so it’s one finished job.
