# Bird Control & Exclusion

Bird Control & Exclusion

## Pest birds on your building? Clear them out — and keep them out.

Pigeons, sparrows, and starlings roosting on ledges, signs, and rooflines leave corrosive droppings, clog vents, and carry mites and disease. Bugsy’s removes the birds and their nests, installs exclusion so they can’t come back, and cleans up the mess they left behind.

## Why pest birds are a problem

A few birds become a flock fast — and the damage adds up on your roof, your signage, and your reputation.

### Corrosive, hazardous droppings

Bird droppings are acidic enough to damage paint, metal, and roofing — and they’re a slip hazard and a health concern for staff and customers.

### Nesting & blockages

Nests clog gutters, vents, and signage, create fire risks around lighting, and bring in secondary pests like bird mites and beetles.

### It only gets worse

Birds return to the same roost year after year. The sooner it’s excluded, the smaller and cheaper the job.

## How it works

### 1. Free estimate & inspection

We identify the species, map the roosting and nesting spots, and recommend the right mix of exclusion and deterrents for your building.

### 2. Remove & deter

We clear active nests and birds and put deterrents in place to break the roosting habit.

### 3. Exclude

Netting, spikes, and proofing seal off ledges, vents, and signage so birds can’t re-establish.

### 4. Clean & sanitize

We clean and sanitize the droppings and debris they left behind, restoring the area safely.
