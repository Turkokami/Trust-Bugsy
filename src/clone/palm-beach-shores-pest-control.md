## The pests Palm Beach Shores faces

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

Surrounded by water and mangroves, the town gets heavy biting-midge and salt-marsh mosquito pressure that peaks around dusk and after king tides.

### Ghost Ants

Salt air and moist landscaping feed fast-spreading ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baths.

### Roaches

American roaches push in from drains, mulch, and seawalls after rain; German roaches breed in kitchens once inside.

### Rodents & Termites

Roof rats seek attics and wall voids, and subterranean termites attack from the moist, sandy soil.
