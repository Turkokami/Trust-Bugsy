## Why the western communities face wetland-edge pressure

The Canyon communities, Aberdeen, and the family and golf neighborhoods of far West Boynton sit against canals, lakes, and preserve land near the Everglades edge. It’s great family territory — and those wild and watery features drive the pest profile.

The lakes and canals breed mosquitoes, the open sunny turf invites fire ants, the broad lawns draw chinch bugs, and the preserve edges push roof rats and other wildlife toward homes. Slab-built homes still need subterranean termite protection from the soil.
