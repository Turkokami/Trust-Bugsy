# Drain Flies

## Drain Flies

Family Psychodidae (moth flies)

Small, fuzzy, moth-like flies that rest on walls near sinks and showers — a near-certain sign that the gelatinous organic film inside a drain or trap has become a breeding ground.

## How to identify them

Drain flies — also called moth flies — are about an eighth of an inch, gray, and covered in fine hairs that give them a fuzzy, moth-like look. They are weak fliers that tend to sit still on walls and ceilings near sinks, showers, and floor drains, fluttering only short distances when disturbed.

## Behavior and biology in South Florida

Drain flies breed in the slimy, gelatinous organic film that builds up inside drains, P-traps, overflow pipes, and around leaks. The larvae feed on that microbial gunk. Because the breeding site is inside the plumbing, the flies appear to come from nowhere and keep returning until the film is physically removed.

Common sources include seldom-used guest bathrooms, floor drains, condensate lines, and drains under equipment. A sudden bloom often points to a specific drain or a slow leak feeding organic matter.

## Signs of an infestation

- Fuzzy moth-like flies resting on walls near sinks, tubs, and floor drains
- Flies emerging from a specific drain (confirmable by taping over it overnight)
- Activity in rarely-used bathrooms or around condensate and floor drains
- Numbers that persist despite surface cleaning

## Why they are a problem

Drain flies are mainly a nuisance and an indicator, but because they develop in the film associated with drains and sewage they can carry bacteria onto surfaces, and heavy indoor populations are a sign of a plumbing or sanitation issue that should be addressed. In rare cases they contribute to indoor air irritation.

## How Bugsy’s treats drain flies

## Preventing drain flies

- Clean drains regularly to remove the organic film larvae feed on
- Run water in seldom-used drains to keep traps from drying and building up scum
- Fix leaks and clear condensate lines
- Scrub or replace dirty garbage-disposal splash guards
- Keep floor drains maintained, especially in laundry and utility areas
