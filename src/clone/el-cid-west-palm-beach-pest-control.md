## Why El Cid’s historic homes have their own pest profile

El Cid, Prospect Park, and Southland Park are among West Palm’s most storied neighborhoods — 1920s Mediterranean-Revival, mission, and frame vernacular homes under a mature oak and ficus canopy, running from Olive Avenue down to Flagler Drive and the Intracoastal. It’s beautiful, and it changes which pests you fight.

Older construction means more gaps: original wood framing and trim invite drywood and subterranean termites, settled foundations and crawlspaces give rodents cover, and the dense mature canopy is a roof-rat highway right to the eaves. The Intracoastal and Flagler Drive waterfront breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums that hit shaded patios and loggias. A modern tract-home routine misses what a ninety-year-old home needs.
