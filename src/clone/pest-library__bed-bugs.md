## What Do Bed Bugs Look Like?

Adult bed bugs are flat, oval, reddish-brown insects about the size of an apple seed — roughly 4–5mm long. After feeding, they become swollen and darker red. Nymphs (juveniles) are smaller and translucent/white when unfed, making them nearly invisible to the naked eye. Bed bugs cannot fly or jump — they crawl.

## Signs of a Bed Bug Infestation

- Rust-colored or dark brown spots on mattress seams, box spring corners, and bedding — crushed bed bugs or fecal matter
- Tiny white eggs (1mm) or translucent shed skins in mattress seams and furniture joints
- Live bed bugs visible in the fold of the mattress, behind the headboard, or in furniture crevices
- Itchy, red bite marks — often in a line, cluster, or zigzag pattern — on arms, legs, neck, or shoulders
- A sweet, musty odor in heavily infested rooms

## Bed Bug Bites — What They Look Like

Bed bug bites are small, raised, red welts that typically appear in clusters or lines. About 30% of people have no visible reaction to bed bug bites, making detection harder. Those who do react may not show symptoms for several days after being bitten. Bite location (shoulders, arms, face) and the line or cluster pattern are more diagnostic than individual bite appearance.

## How Bed Bugs Spread

Per [EPA guidance](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol), bed bugs spread exclusively through human activity — they don’t fly or jump to new locations on their own. Common sources include: hotel stays (bags left on luggage racks or floors), secondhand furniture or mattresses, guests bringing them on clothing or bags, and unit-to-unit spread in apartments and condos through wall voids and shared plumbing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I see bed bugs with the naked eye?
Adults yes — they’re the size of an apple seed and visible in good lighting. Nymphs and eggs are much harder to see. A thorough inspection by a professional with a flashlight and magnification is the most reliable way to confirm an infestation.

### Does a clean home prevent bed bugs?
No. Bed bugs don’t care about cleanliness — they care about access to a human blood host. They’re found in five-star hotels as frequently as lower-budget accommodations. Anyone who travels or purchases secondhand goods is at risk.

### Should I throw away my mattress?
Rarely necessary. Professional treatment of the mattress, box spring, bed frame, and surrounding furniture is usually sufficient. Throwing away a mattress without treating the room is ineffective — bed bugs live in walls, furniture, and flooring, not just the mattress.

### How do bed bugs spread in apartments?
Bed bugs travel through wall voids, electrical conduit, and plumbing between units. An infestation in one apartment can spread to multiple units within weeks. Building-wide inspection and treatment is often necessary in multi-unit properties.
