## Why Restaurant Pest Control in South Florida Is Non-Negotiable

South Florida’s year-round heat and humidity create constant pest pressure that doesn’t slow down between seasons. Commercial kitchens offer everything pests need: food, water, warmth, and shelter. The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) requires all food service establishments to maintain pest-free conditions — and inspectors don’t give advance notice. A failed health inspection can trigger temporary closure, fines, and a public record that follows your business online for years. Proactive, scheduled pest control for restaurants in Palm Beach County and Broward County is not just a compliance tool — it’s a business protection strategy.

## Common Pests in Restaurant & Food Service Environments

- Roof rats & house mice — Contaminate dry storage, chew through packaging, and leave droppings in prep areas. Learn about our
- Ghost ants & fire ants — Invade prep stations, sugar storage, and outdoor seating areas year-round in South Florida. See our
- Fruit flies & drain flies — Breed in floor drains, mop buckets, produce storage, and bar areas — a persistent problem in Florida’s humidity.
- Filth flies — Spread bacteria from dumpster areas into kitchen environments through door gaps and loading docks.
- Stored product beetles & moths — Infest flour, rice, spices, and dry goods — common in high-volume kitchens with bulk storage.

Answers to Your Pest Control Questions

### How often does a restaurant in South Florida need pest control?
Most Florida restaurants require monthly service at minimum to maintain DBPR compliance. High-volume kitchens or establishments with previous pest activity may benefit from bi-monthly visits. We assess your facility and recommend the right frequency.

### Will your treatments affect food safety or require closing?
No. We use food-safe, EPA-registered treatments applied after service hours or in non-food zones. We coordinate with your kitchen schedule so service never disrupts operations.

### Do you provide documentation for DBPR health inspections?
Yes. Every service visit includes a written report documenting products used, treatment locations, pest activity observed, and recommendations — ready for DBPR review on request.

### What if cockroaches return between scheduled visits?
Our commercial service includes a pest-free guarantee. If pests return between visits, we come back and retreat at no additional charge.
