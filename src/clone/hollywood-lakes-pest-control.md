## Why Hollywood Lakes homes need year-round coverage

Hollywood Lakes is the city’s prized historic district — gracious older homes on tree-lined streets between North Lake, South Lake, and the Intracoastal. The water breeds no-see-ums and mosquitoes, the seawalls draw iguanas, and the mature canopy shelters roof rats.

Older construction also faces termites in the warm, moist soil. A discreet, thorough plan tuned to a historic waterfront home keeps it all in check.

## The pests Hollywood Lakes homes face

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

The beach, Intracoastal, and mangrove edges breed biting midges and mosquitoes that swarm patios, balconies, and pool decks at dawn and dusk.

### Iguanas & Wildlife

Seawalls and canal banks are prime iguana habitat — they burrow, damage landscaping, and foul docks and pools.

### Roof Rats

Mature tropical landscaping and condo chases give roof rats routes into attics, soffits, and units.

### Palmetto Bugs & Termites

American roaches push up from sewers and drains after rain, while subterranean termites attack from the moist, sandy soil.
