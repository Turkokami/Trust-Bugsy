## The pests Bear Lakes homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Golf-course lakes are prime breeding grounds; mosquitoes and midges hit lakefront and fairway homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Moist, maintained landscaping feeds big multi-queen ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches move in from mulch, drains, and lake edges after rain, appearing in garages and lanais.

### Roof Rats & Termites

Mature trees give roof rats a path to attics; subterranean termites work up from the soil under slabs.
