## Why you keep seeing palmetto bugs — even in a clean home

The big roaches South Floridians call palmetto bugs are American roaches, and they overwhelmingly breed in the warm, damp dark of the sewer and storm-drain system. From there they travel up your main line and slip in through drains, cleanouts, and plumbing gaps — which is why a spotless kitchen still gets them, especially after heavy rain.

Spraying inside only treats the roaches you can see. It doesn’t touch the source in the line, so they keep coming back. To actually stop them, you have to intercept them where they’re coming from.
