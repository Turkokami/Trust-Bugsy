## Why the western estates face wetland-edge pressure

Mirasol, Old Palm, and the Gardens’ other western golf-estate communities put luxury homes around championship courses and lakes out toward the Loxahatchee Slough and preserve land. It’s the most exclusive western living, and the courses plus the wetland edge drive the heaviest pest pressure in the city.

The course lakes and the Slough breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums all season; the open turf invites fire ants; and the preserve and Slough edges push roof rats, raccoons, coyotes, and other wildlife toward homes. Big estate lots mean more perimeter, roofline, and landscaping to protect.
