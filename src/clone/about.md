**The Bugsy’s Story:** From Marine to Pest Control Expert

**A Mission-Driven Beginning** My journey into pest control began in July 2017, following my service in the United States Marine Corps. Transitioning from military life, I quickly realized my passion for problem-solving and protecting homes, leading me to an apprenticeship in the pest control industry.

**Learning, Observing, and Improving** During my apprenticeship, I identified gaps in the industry—lack of technician dedication due to poor compensation and outdated training methods in large corporations. I knew there was a better way to elevate pest management.

**The Pursuit of Excellence** Determined to make a change, I took matters into my own hands, furthering my education at the University of Florida’s Pest Management University. Here, I gained new insights, refined my expertise, and developed a more effective and science-backed approach to pest control.

Answers to Your Pest Control Questions

### How often should I schedule pest control services?
Every home is different, but most of our customers benefit from quarterly pest control treatments to stay protected all year. After a complete inspection, we’ll recommend a service plan that fits your needs, whether that’s one-time, monthly, bi-monthly, or quarterly.

### What areas does Bugsy's Pest Solutions serve?
We proudly serve homes and businesses across South Florida, including Lake Worth Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, West Palm Beach, and nearby communities. If you’re not sure if you’re in our service area, just reach out — we’re happy to help!

### Are your pest control treatments safe for children and pets?
Yes, 100%. At Trust Bugsy, we put the safety of your family and pets first. We use eco-friendly, low-impact treatments that are tough on pests but gentle on your home. Every product we apply is carefully selected to keep your loved ones safe while delivering real results.

### What makes Bugsy's Pest Solutions different from other pest control companies?
Trust Bugsy was built on honesty, service, and real results. As a veteran-owned and family-operated business, we take pride in offering science-backed pest control without scare tactics, hidden fees, or cookie-cutter solutions. We show up on time, customize every treatment, and always put your safety and satisfaction first.

### How quickly can Trust Bugsy schedule my pest control service?
We know pests don’t wait — and neither should you. That’s why Trust Bugsy works hard to schedule most services within 24 to 48 hours. Just give us a call or request a free estimate online, and we’ll get you on the calendar as soon as possible.
