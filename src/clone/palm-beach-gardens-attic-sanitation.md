## Why attic cleanup is part of finishing a rodent job

Getting the rodents out is only half the job. Roof rats leave behind droppings, urine, and contaminated, matted insulation — and the pheromone trails in that mess actively signal the next rodent that your Palm Beach Gardens attic is a good place to live.

Cleaning and sanitizing the attic removes the health hazard and the scent trail, which is why we treat sanitation as the step that finishes the job — not an optional add-on. It pairs directly with trapping and exclusion.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my attic need sanitizing after rodents?
Rodents leave droppings, urine, and pheromone trails that are both a health hazard and a signal that draws the next rodent in. Sanitizing removes both.

### Do you remove and replace the insulation?
Yes. We remove insulation that rodents have contaminated or matted down and can replace it so your attic performs and insulates properly again.

### Is the cleanup safe and thorough?
We follow careful, health-conscious cleanup practices to remove contamination and disinfect the space, and we’ll walk you through what was done.

### Should I do exclusion at the same time?
Yes — sanitizing without sealing the entry points invites re-infestation, so we coordinate cleanup with rodent trapping and exclusion.

### Can this help with attic odor?
Yes. Deodorizing and pheromone decontamination are part of the service, which addresses the smell rodents leave behind.
