## Why Indian Spring homes need coverage

Indian Spring is an established gated golf community built around two courses and a chain of lakes, with mature landscaping throughout. The lakes breed mosquitoes and midges, and the irrigated grounds feed ant colonies that trail indoors.

As an established community, many homes have settled enough to open small gaps where ants, roaches, and rodents slip in. We treat the source and seal the entries rather than chasing trails.

## The pests Indian Spring homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Golf-course lakes and water features are prime breeding grounds; mosquitoes and midges hit lake- and fairway-front homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Moist, maintained landscaping feeds big multi-queen colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches move in from mulch, drains, and lake edges after rain, showing up in garages and lanais.

### Roof Rats & Termites

Mature landscaping gives roof rats a route to attics; subterranean termites attack from the soil under slabs.
