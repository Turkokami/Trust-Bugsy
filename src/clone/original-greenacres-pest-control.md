## The pests older Greenacres homes face

### Roof Rats & Rodents

Mature trees put roof rats onto roofs and into attics and soffits; settled homes give them gaps to exploit.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Moist landscaping and older slabs feed multi-queen ant colonies that trail into kitchens and baths.

### Palmetto Bugs & Roaches

American roaches push in from drains and mulch after rain; German roaches breed fast in older kitchens.

### Termites

Subterranean termites attack from the soil under aging slabs, and drywood termites swarm in spring — older homes are prime targets.
