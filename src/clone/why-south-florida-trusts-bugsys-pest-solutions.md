### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What should I do if I suspect a pest infestation in my home?

If you suspect pests, act quickly. Note where you see activity and what kind of pest it appears to be. Avoid DIY pesticides that may not solve the problem and can pose risks. Instead, call a professional like Bugsy’s for an inspection — we identify the source, explain options, and recommend a safe, targeted plan.

#### How often should I schedule pest control services?

Service frequency depends on the pest, property, and local conditions. Many homeowners find quarterly visits prevent most problems, but some situations require monthly or seasonal attention. We’ll assess your home and recommend a schedule that matches your risk and goals.

#### Are the eco-friendly methods used by Bugsy’s effective?

Yes. Our eco-conscious approach focuses on prevention, monitoring, and targeted treatments. IPM combines those steps with low-impact products and habitat changes so pest populations drop without unnecessary chemical use. Many customers see long-term success with this strategy.

#### What should I expect during a pest control treatment?

Expect a full property inspection, a clear explanation of findings, and a treatment plan. Depending on the issue, we may apply low-impact products, set traps, or use exclusion techniques. Our technicians will explain any precautions and follow-up needed so you know what to expect.

#### How can I prevent pests from entering my home?

Seal cracks and gaps around doors, windows, and utility lines. Keep food sealed, clean up crumbs, and store garbage properly. Regular cleaning and routine inspections help, and professional attic insulation or sanitation can remove nesting sites and close entry points.

#### What are the signs of a termite infestation?

Watch for mud tubes on foundations, hollow-sounding wood, or discarded wings near windows and doors. You might also see tiny wood-colored pellets (frass). If you suspect termites, contact a pro immediately — Bugsy’s offers thorough termite inspections and treatments to protect your home.
