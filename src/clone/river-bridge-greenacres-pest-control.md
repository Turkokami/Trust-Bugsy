## Why River Bridge sees steady pest activity

River Bridge is a large gated community built around a golf course and a network of lakes, with mature landscaping throughout. Those water features and lush yards are exactly what mosquitoes, midges, and ants need — and homes on the fairways or lakes feel it first.

With a mix of single-family homes and townhomes sharing landscaping and irrigation, the soil stays moist year-round, feeding ant colonies and giving roaches and rodents easy harborage. A plan matched to your street keeps it controlled.

## The pests River Bridge homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

The golf-course lakes are prime breeding grounds; mosquitoes and midges hit lakefront and fairway homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Moist, maintained landscaping feeds big multi-queen colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches move in from mulch, drains, and lake edges after rain, appearing in garages and lanais.

### Roof Rats & Termites

Mature trees give roof rats a path to attics; subterranean termites work up from the soil under slabs.
