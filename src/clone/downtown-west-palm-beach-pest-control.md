## Why downtown’s density defines its pests

Downtown West Palm — Clematis Street, Rosemary Square, the Flagler Drive high-rises, and the waterfront condos — is the densest, most mixed-use square mile in the county, wrapped along the Intracoastal. That concentration of restaurants, retail, offices, and residences over shared structure is exactly what urban pests exploit.

German cockroaches ride shared walls, plumbing chases, and constant food deliveries between units and businesses; roof rats and mice work alleys, dumpster corrals, and service areas; and the Intracoastal along Flagler breeds mosquitoes and no-see-ums that reach balconies and rooftop terraces many stories up. A high-rise condo and a Clematis restaurant need coordinated, discreet, documented service.
