## Why termites love Boca Raton

Termites need warmth, moisture, and wood — and Boca Raton offers all three year-round. Our frost-free climate lets colonies stay active every month, and the mix of East Boca’s older Golden Triangle homes and slab-built homes gives termites countless ways in.

There are two very different threats here, treated differently. **Subterranean termites** (including aggressive Formosan termites) live in the soil and enter through foundations and slabs — often invisibly. **Drywood termites** live entirely inside dry structural wood, framing, and attics, with no soil contact. Spring is peak swarm season, when both send out winged reproductives — and when many homeowners first discover a problem.

## The two termites we treat

Correct identification drives the right treatment — they are not the same job.

### Subterranean & Formosan Termites

Soil-dwelling termites that enter from the ground through foundations and slabs, often building mud tubes. Formosan colonies are especially large and destructive.

### Drywood Termites

Live entirely inside dry wood — framing, trim, attics, and furniture — especially common in Boca Raton’s older homes. Signs include pellet-like droppings (frass) and discarded wings.

### What are the signs of termites?
Watch for mud tubes on foundations (subterranean), pellet-like frass (drywood), discarded wings near windows after a swarm, hollow-sounding or blistered wood, and sagging floors and trim.
