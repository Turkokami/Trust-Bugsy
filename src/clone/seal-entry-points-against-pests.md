## Common Entry Points: Where Pests Invade Florida Homes

### Foundation and Structural Vulnerabilities

Florida’s **sandy soil** and frequent weather changes cause foundations to shift, creating perfect pest entry points:

#### Primary Foundation Threats:

- **Hairline cracks** in stucco and concrete block walls
- **Expansion joints** between building materials
- **Settlement gaps** around basement or crawl space areas
- **Separation points** between foundation and siding

**Pro Tip:** Even cracks as thin as a credit card allow **ant colonies** to establish highways into your home.

### Door and Window Weak Points

Florida’s **intense UV exposure** degrades weatherstripping faster than any other climate:

#### Critical Sealing Areas:

- **Worn door sweeps** creating gaps under entry doors
- **Damaged weatherstripping** around window frames
- **Missing caulk** around glass panes and sills
- **Warped frames** from heat and humidity exposure

### Utility and Service Line Penetrations

These **often-overlooked areas** serve as superhighways for pests:

#### High-Risk Penetrations:

- **Air conditioning lines** and electrical conduits
- **Cable, internet, and phone** line entry points
- **Water and gas meter** connections
- **Dryer vents** and exhaust fan openings
- **Outdoor spigots** and irrigation connections

## DIY Entry Point Sealing: Step-by-Step Guide

While **professional pest control services** provide comprehensive protection, homeowners can address basic sealing needs:

### Essential Tools and Materials:

- **High-quality caulk gun** for precise application
- **UV-resistant caulks** rated for outdoor use
- **Weatherstripping** appropriate for your door/window types
- **Expanding foam** (use sparingly – less is more)
- **Copper mesh** for rodent-proofing larger gaps

### Step-by-Step Process:

#### Week 1: Foundation Level Sealing

- **Clean all surfaces** with degreasing solution
- **Apply caulk** in continuous beads without air pockets
- **Tool smooth** within 10 minutes of application
- **Allow full cure** before moving to next area

#### Week 2: Window and Door Sealing

- **Remove old weatherstripping** completely
- **Clean channels** thoroughly before installation
- **Install new materials** with proper compression
- **Test operation** ensuring smooth function

#### Week 3: Utility Penetration Sealing

- **Photograph locations** before beginning work
- **Use appropriate materials** for each specific gap
- **Avoid over-application** which can cause problems
- **Label sealed areas** for future reference

### Common DIY Mistakes to Avoid:

- **Using wrong caulk types** for specific applications
- **Over-applying expanding foam** causing structural issues
- **Sealing drainage points** causing water damage
- **Ignoring building code requirements** for ventilation
