## Why South Florida Flea Infestations Are Worse

According to [UF IFAS Extension](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/), Florida’s climate allows flea populations to remain active and breeding for 10–12 months of the year. The cat flea (Ctenocephalides felis) is the dominant species in South Florida and infests both cats and dogs. Even indoor-only pets can get fleas from wildlife like opossums, squirrels, and rodents that pass through your yard.

## Signs of a Flea Infestation

- Pets scratching, biting, or grooming excessively
- Small jumping insects visible in carpet, on pets, or on light-colored socks
- Tiny dark specks (flea dirt — feces) in pet bedding or on pet’s skin
- Red, itchy bite marks on ankles and lower legs (humans)
- Pets becoming unusually restless or uncomfortable
- Flea eggs (tiny white specks) in carpet fibers or pet bedding

## Our Flea Treatment Process

### Interior Treatment

We apply a combination of residual insecticide and insect growth regulator (IGR) to all floor surfaces, carpets, baseboards, and upholstered furniture. The IGR is critical — it prevents flea eggs and larvae from developing into breeding adults, breaking the infestation cycle. This is the step consumer products skip.

### Yard Treatment

For outdoor flea pressure, we treat lawn areas, landscaping beds, and shaded zones where fleas and wildlife concentrate. A yard flea treatment without interior treatment — or vice versa — will not fully resolve an established infestation.

### Follow-Up Treatment

Flea pupae (the cocoon stage) are protected from insecticides and can remain dormant for weeks. A follow-up treatment 2–3 weeks after the initial service targets newly emerged fleas before they can breed — completing the elimination process.

##  What You Should Do Before Flea Treatment

- Vacuum all floor surfaces thoroughly and dispose of the bag/canister contents outside
- Wash all pet bedding on the hottest dryer cycle
- Have your pets treated with a veterinarian-recommended flea preventative on the day of service
- Mow the lawn before yard treatment
- Clear floors of loose items — toys, shoes, clothing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get fleas without having pets?
Yes. Fleas can enter your home via rodents, wildlife, or even on your clothing. If you recently moved into a home with a prior pet history, dormant flea eggs and pupae in the carpet can hatch weeks later.

### My pet is on flea prevention — why do I still have fleas?
Pet flea preventatives protect the pet but don’t treat the home environment. Flea eggs, larvae, and pupae in carpets and furniture will continue producing new adults regardless of what the pet is on. Both the pet AND the home need to be treated.

### How long after treatment will I still see fleas?
You may see live fleas for up to 2 weeks after treatment — these are newly emerged adults from pupae that survived the initial treatment. This is normal and expected. The follow-up treatment handles this.
