## The pests Saddle Trail Park estates face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Canals, wet pastures, and the nearby preserves breed biting insects that torment horses and people alike at dawn and dusk.

### Stable & Filth Flies

Barns, manure, and feed rooms draw house flies, stable flies, and bottle flies that plague both the barn and the home.

### Fire Ants & Rodents

Open turf and paddocks are ideal for fire-ant mounds, while feed and tack rooms invite roof rats and mice.

### Termites & Palmetto Bugs

Subterranean termites attack from moist soil under homes and barns; palmetto bugs push in from mulch after rain.
