## Ant Control in Jupiter

Jupiter’s proximity to natural areas means heavy ant pressure from ghost ants, carpenter ants, and fire ants. [UF IFAS Extension](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/) research identifies ghost ants as the dominant structural ant pest throughout South Florida. Bugsy’s [ant control](/ant-control/) uses non-repellent baiting technology to eliminate the entire colony — not just the workers you can see. For outdoor fire ant pressure near Jupiter’s parks and greenways, we apply broadcast granular bait treatments.

## Termite Control in Jupiter

Jupiter’s wooden structures and lush landscaping create ideal conditions for both drywood and subterranean termites. Subterranean termite colonies in Jupiter’s sandy soil can contain millions of foragers. The [EPA termite identification guide](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol/termites-how-identify-and-control-them) recommends annual inspections for all South Florida properties. Bugsy’s [termite control](/termite-control/) includes liquid barrier treatments, bait station monitoring, and fumigation for severe infestations — all backed by warranty.
