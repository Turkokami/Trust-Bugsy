## The pests Harbor Beach estates face

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

Canals, the Intracoastal, and mangrove edges breed biting midges and mosquitoes that swarm docks, patios, and pool decks at dawn and dusk.

### Iguanas & Wildlife

Seawalls and canal banks are prime iguana habitat — they burrow, damage landscaping, and foul docks and pools.

### Roof Rats

Mature tropical landscaping and canopy give roof rats a direct route into attics, soffits, and outdoor kitchens.

### Palmetto Bugs & Termites

American roaches push up from sewers and drains after rain, while subterranean termites attack from the warm, moist soil.
