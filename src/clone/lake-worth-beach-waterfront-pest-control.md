## Why waterfront living means different pests

Lake Worth Beach’s waterfront — the Lagoon-front blocks around Bryant Park and the beachside homes and condos on the barrier island across the bridge — sits against seawalls, docks, and open water. It’s prized living, and it reshapes which pests you fight.

Seawalls, docks, and dense waterfront landscaping give roof rats continuous elevated cover to the roofline. The Lagoon and the ocean breed mosquitoes and, especially, **no-see-ums** that come off the water at dawn and dusk onto docks, balconies, and Bryant Park itself. Standard inland routes miss what a waterfront home needs.
