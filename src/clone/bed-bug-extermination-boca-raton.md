### **Can Bed Bugs Bite Through Clothes, Sheets, or Socks?**

![A close up shot of bed bugs on top a bed sheet.](https://trustbugsy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/close-up-shot-of-bed-bugs-in-a-bed-sheet.webp)

**Yes, bed bugs can bite through thin fabrics**, like **sheets, socks, and lightweight clothing**. While they prefer **direct skin contact**, they can crawl under clothes and **bite exposed skin areas** like arms, legs, and torso.

**They cannot truly bite “through” thicker fabrics**, but they’ll **find a way to reach your skin**. That’s why **protective mattress encasements** and **professional treatments** are key to prevention.

### **How Long Can Bed Bugs Live Without Feeding or in Plastic?**

Here are some **quick facts** you should know:

- **Bed bugs can survive 4-6 months without a blood meal** under normal conditions.
- In some cases, **bed bugs have survived over a year** in cooler environments.
- **Bed bugs can’t thrive on plastic surfaces** (like containers), but they can **hide in small crevices or seams** in plastic objects.
- **Sealing infested items in plastic bags** can help control an infestation, but **it’s not a complete solution**.

- **Dark spots** (fecal matter) on mattresses, sheets, or walls.
