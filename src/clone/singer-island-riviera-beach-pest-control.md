## Why Singer Island homes and condos need coverage

Singer Island sits between the Atlantic and the Intracoastal, ringed by mangroves and canals and topped with oceanfront high-rises. The mangroves and tidal flats breed no-see-ums and salt-marsh mosquitoes that swarm at dawn and dusk, while the sea air and water are constant.

Roof rats travel dock lines, landscaping, and building chases into attics and upper-floor units alike.

Ghost ants trail indoors through the humid season, and iguanas colonize seawalls, canal banks, and landscaping.
