Cockroaches are one of the most resilient pests on the planet — and South Florida’s heat and humidity are ideal for year-round roach activity. Whether you’re dealing with palmetto bugs (American cockroaches) invading from outside or a German cockroach infestation spreading through your kitchen, [Bugsy’s Pest Solutions](/) has the professional-grade tools and techniques to eliminate them completely.

## ![Technician treating a garage interior with an extension pole duster](https://trustbugsy.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/5.webp) Types of Cockroaches in South Florida

### German Cockroaches

German cockroaches are the most difficult roach to eliminate and the #1 cockroach problem in South Florida kitchens, restaurants, and apartments. A single female produces up to 300 offspring in her lifetime. They develop resistance to generic sprays rapidly. Per [UF IFAS Extension](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/) research, professional-grade gel baiting combined with insect growth regulators (IGRs) is the most effective control method.

### American Cockroaches (Palmetto Bugs)

Palmetto bugs are large (up to 2 inches), fast, and one of South Florida’s most infamous pests. They primarily live outdoors in trees, mulch, and drainage areas — but regularly enter homes through drains, garage doors, and exterior gaps. Learn more on our [palmetto bug guide](https://trustbugsy.com/pest-library/palmetto-bugs/).

### Smoky Brown Cockroaches

Smoky brown cockroaches are common in South Florida’s humid coastal areas. They’re strong fliers and are often found in mulch beds, tree holes, and roof gutters before entering homes.

### Brown-Banded Cockroaches

Brown-banded cockroaches prefer warm, dry areas away from moisture — they’re often found in bedrooms, closets, and electronics rather than the kitchen. They require a different treatment strategy than moisture-loving species.

## Why Generic Sprays Don’t Work on German Cockroaches

- German cockroaches develop resistance to pyrethroids (the active ingredient in most consumer sprays) within a few generations
- Sprays repel roaches into hiding rather than eliminating colonies
- Consumer products don’t reach hidden harborage areas inside walls, appliances, and cabinets
- Without insect growth regulators, new generations continue hatching from egg cases

## Bugsy’s Professional Cockroach Treatment

### Professional Gel Bait

We apply professional-grade gel bait in cracks, crevices, and harborage areas where cockroaches hide. Cockroaches consume the bait and pass the active ingredient to nestmates through contact and feces — eliminating the entire population progressively.

### Insect Growth Regulators (IGRs)

IGRs break the cockroach breeding cycle by preventing nymphs from developing into reproductive adults. This is the critical step that consumer products skip — and it’s why professional treatment produces permanent results while store-bought sprays don’t.

### Void Treatments

For established infestations, we treat wall voids, behind appliances, and under cabinets with borate dust or residual liquid — reaching the harborage areas where cockroaches spend 75% of their time.

### Perimeter Barrier for Palmetto Bugs

For American cockroaches entering from outside, we apply a residual perimeter treatment around the foundation, entry points, and drainage areas to stop them before they get inside.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to get rid of German cockroaches?
With professional bait and IGR treatment, most infestations show significant reduction within 1–2 weeks. Complete elimination typically takes 3–6 weeks depending on the severity. Follow-up service is often recommended for severe infestations.

### I only see one or two roaches — do I have an infestation?
Cockroaches are nocturnal and hide extremely well. If you see even one during the day, there is almost certainly a much larger hidden population. A single visible roach is almost always a sign of an established infestation.

### Is your cockroach treatment safe for my family?
Yes. Professional gel baits are applied in targeted areas out of reach of children and pets. We use EPA-registered products specifically selected for residential and commercial safety.

### What do I need to do to prepare for cockroach treatment?
Remove items from under sinks and from kitchen cabinet shelves. Clean countertops but do not spray any consumer insecticides for at least 48 hours before treatment — they can repel cockroaches away from the bait.
