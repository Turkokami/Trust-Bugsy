## Why western WPB faces wetland-edge pressure

Western West Palm Beach — the gated golf and family communities like Ibis, Andros Isle, and Baywinds, and the neighborhoods stretching west along Okeechobee and Forest Hill toward the Grassy Waters Preserve and Clear Lake — is defined by community lakes, canals, and preserve land at the Everglades edge. Every one of those features is also a pest driver.

The lakes, canals, and Grassy Waters breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums all wet season; the open sunny turf and broad St. Augustine lawns invite fire ants and chinch bugs; and the preserve edges push roof rats, raccoons, and other wildlife toward homes. Slab-built homes still face subterranean termites from the soil. This is the county’s heaviest mosquito-and-wildlife territory, and it needs a program made for it.
