## Why East Boca’s older homes need older-home-aware care

East Boca — the Golden Triangle and the neighborhoods between the Intracoastal and A1A — is defined by older single-family homes, mature trees, and salt-air humidity. It’s a wonderful, established part of the city, and those features drive a specific pest profile.

Aged structural wood makes **drywood termites** a headline threat — they live entirely inside framing and trim with no soil contact, and decades-old homes give them abundant harborage. Older soffits and rooflines let roof rats in, the Intracoastal seawalls give them a highway, and the humidity and lush landscaping keep ghost and white-footed ants trailing indoors.

## The pests that define East Boca

Older homes, mature wood, and near-water humidity set the profile.

### Drywood Termites

The top risk for East Boca’s older homes — look for pellet-like frass and discarded wings. We favor no-tent BoraCare and spot treatment. See our termite page.

### Roof Rats

Older soffits and Intracoastal seawalls give rats easy access. We trap and seal the exterior openings.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Humidity and lush landscaping keep tiny ants trailing across kitchens and baths — baited, not sprayed.

### Subterranean Termites

Settling and additions can open soil-contact paths on older homes; we inspect for both termite types.

## East Boca & the Golden Triangle pest control — questions we hear

### Why is my East Boca home at higher termite risk?
East Boca has many older homes with aged structural wood, ideal for drywood termites (inside the framing) and subterranean termites (from the soil). Both are common here and treated differently, which is why we inspect and identify first.

### Do I have to tent my older home for termites?
Usually not. For drywood termites we favor targeted no-tent BoraCare and spot treatments — no fumigation, no bagging food, no leaving your home — which is gentler on an older structure.

### How are rats getting into my near-water home?
Older soffits and roofline gaps, plus the Intracoastal seawalls and mature trees, give roof rats easy elevated access. We trap and seal those exterior entry points.

### Are treatments safe for kids and pets?
Yes. Our programs use targeted, low-impact methods with clear guidance on any short re-entry intervals.
