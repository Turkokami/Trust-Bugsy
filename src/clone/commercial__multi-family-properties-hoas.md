In apartment complexes, condominiums, and HOA communities, a pest problem in one unit almost never stays in one unit. German cockroaches, bed bugs, and rodents travel freely through shared wall voids, plumbing chases, and common areas — turning a single tenant’s complaint into a building-wide infestation without a systematic, coordinated response.

Bugsy’s Pest Solutions provides apartment pest control, condominium pest control, and HOA pest control programs throughout Palm Beach, Broward, Martin & St. Lucie counties. We coordinate with property management teams, work around tenant schedules, and provide the documentation you need to stay compliant with Florida landlord-tenant law and keep residents satisfied.

## Why Building-Wide Programs Are Essential for Multi-Family Pest Control

Individual unit treatments only address part of the problem. Pests in multi-family buildings exploit shared infrastructure — plumbing stacks, electrical conduits, wall voids, HVAC systems — to move freely between units. Without a coordinated program that covers common areas, trash rooms, laundry facilities, and the building perimeter, infestations continue to cycle regardless of how many individual units get treated. Florida landlord-tenant law also places clear obligations on property owners to maintain habitable, pest-free conditions. A documented, proactive South Florida apartment pest control program protects tenants, reduces your legal liability, and keeps lease renewal rates where they need to be.

Answers to Your Pest Control Questions

### How do you coordinate service access with tenants?
We work with your property management team to provide required advance notice to tenants, in compliance with Florida landlord-tenant law. We can also provide tenant-facing summaries of what was done and why.

### Can you handle a bed bug situation in a multi-unit building?
Yes. Our multi-family bed bug program includes inspection of affected and adjacent units, targeted treatment, and follow-up monitoring to confirm elimination and prevent spread to neighboring units.

### How do you stop German cockroaches from coming back?
We use targeted gel bait programs and exclusion work to address harborage areas and inter-unit pathways — not broadcast sprays. Recurring scheduled service prevents population rebuilding over time.

### Do you offer discounts for whole-building contracts?
Yes. We offer competitive pricing for building-wide service agreements. Contact us to discuss a program tailored to your property size and unit count.
