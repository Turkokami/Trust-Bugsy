Victoria Grove’s newer, tightly-planned streets off Southern Boulevard sit among community lakes and fresh landscaping. ## The pests Victoria Grove homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Community lakes and retention ponds keep mosquitoes and midges breeding, hitting homes near the water hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost Ants

Fresh, irrigated landscaping is perfect for fast-spreading ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### German Roaches & Palmetto Bugs

German roaches ride in and breed in kitchens; American roaches push in from mulch and drains after rain.

### Rodents & Termites

Rodents seek warm wall voids as nights cool, and subterranean termites attack from the soil even under new slabs.
