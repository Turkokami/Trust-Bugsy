## The pests that define Greenacres

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Canals and community lakes across Greenacres breed mosquitoes and biting midges that swarm at dawn and dusk, worst on waterfront lots.

### Ants

Ghost and white-footed ants build multi-queen colonies in the moist landscaping and trail indoors to kitchens and baths.

### Palmetto Bugs & Roaches

American roaches surge from drains, mulch, and canals after rain; German roaches breed fast in kitchens once inside.

### Rodents & Termites

Roof rats move through the tree canopy into attics, and subterranean termites attack from the soil under slabs.
