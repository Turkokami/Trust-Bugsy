## Two roaches, two completely different problems

The large brown roaches Floridians call “palmetto bugs” are American cockroaches — and they mostly live **outdoors**, in mulch, sewers, tree holes, and landscaping, flying or wandering indoors on warm, humid nights. Seeing one does not mean your home is dirty; it means Delray’s climate and dense planting are doing what they always do. Control focuses on the exterior harborage and sealing entry points.

**German cockroaches** are the opposite: small roaches that breed rapidly **indoors**, hiding in kitchens and bathrooms and spreading between shared-wall units in Delray’s many condos and villas. They can’t be sprayed away — they require strategic baiting and sanitation, and in multi-unit buildings, coordination between units.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do I see big roaches even though my house is clean?

Those are palmetto bugs (American cockroaches), and they live outdoors in mulch, sewers, and landscaping, wandering or flying in on warm nights. They reflect Delray’s climate, not cleanliness, and are controlled by treating the exterior and sealing entry points.

### How do you get rid of German roaches in a condo?

German roaches require strategic baiting and sanitation, not sprays — and because they travel between shared-wall units, we coordinate treatment across affected units so the problem doesn’t just move next door and come back.

### Why won’t store-bought sprays fix my roach problem?

Sprays kill the roaches you see but miss the hidden breeding population, and for German roaches they can even scatter the infestation. Targeted baiting reaches the whole population, which is why we rely on it.

### Are roaches a health concern?

Yes — roaches can contaminate food and surfaces and their droppings and shed skins are a known asthma and allergy trigger, which is why prompt, thorough control matters.
