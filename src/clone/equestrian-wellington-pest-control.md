## Why a working equestrian property has its own pest profile

The Equestrian Preserve around the Wellington show grounds — the barns, paddocks, feed rooms, and estate homes of the horse world — concentrates exactly the conditions pests want: stored feed and hay, manure and muck piles, water troughs, wash racks, and ponds, all busiest in the winter season.

Stable flies and house flies breed in manure and bedding; rodents raid feed and tack rooms; fire ants and mosquitoes colonize paddocks and pond edges; and the estate homes themselves face the usual South Florida pests plus everything the barns attract. Every treatment here has to be safe around horses, dogs, and barn cats.

## The pests we target on horse properties

Feed, manure, water, and ponds set the profile.

### Stable & House Flies

Manure, bedding, and muck breed flies that torment horses — managed with baiting, traps, and source reduction, horse-safe.

### Rodents

Feed and tack rooms are a magnet for rats and mice — trapped and excluded around barns and feed storage.

### Fire Ants & Mosquitoes

Paddocks and pond edges breed both — treated with barrier and mound work that keeps horses and riders comfortable.

### Ants & Roaches in the Home

The estate residence still needs standard interior protection — baited and treated at the source.

## Everything we use is horse- and pet-safe.

On a working farm, safety is non-negotiable. We build barn and paddock programs around targeted, low-impact methods and careful placement so horses, dogs, and barn cats are protected while the pests are not.
