## Why the marina core has its own pest profile

Downtown Boynton and the Marina District pack mixed-use buildings, restaurants, and older homes tightly against the Boynton Harbor Marina and the Intracoastal. That density plus water at the edge is exactly what pests exploit.

German cockroaches travel shared walls and deliveries in the mixed-use blocks, rodents work the older buildings and dumpster areas, ants trail in from waterfront landscaping, and the marina and Intracoastal breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums that hit patios and balconies.
