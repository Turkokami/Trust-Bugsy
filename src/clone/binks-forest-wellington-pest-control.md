## Why golf-community living drives these pests

Binks Forest and Wellington’s other golf communities wrap homes around courses, water features, and mature landscaping, out toward the preserve and canal edges. It’s prized living, and every one of those features is also a pest driver.

The course lakes and water features breed mosquitoes all season; the open sunny turf invites fire ants and chinch bugs; the mature landscaping shelters ants and gives roof rats cover; and the preserve and canal edges push wildlife toward homes. Slab-built homes still face subterranean termites from the soil.
