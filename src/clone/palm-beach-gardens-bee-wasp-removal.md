## The stinging insects we handle in Palm Beach Gardens

Palm Beach Gardens’s warm climate keeps stinging insects active nearly year-round. The most common calls are paper wasps building under eaves and soffits, yellowjackets nesting in the ground or wall voids, and hornets in trees and shrubs — all of which defend their nests aggressively.

A nest near a door, patio, pool cage, or play area is a genuine hazard, and knocking one down yourself often ends in stings. We treat and remove nests safely and knock down the activity at the source.

## What we treat — and what we advise on

### Paper Wasps

The most common nest — open, umbrella-shaped combs under eaves, soffits, and rails. Treated and removed safely.

### Yellowjackets

Aggressive ground- and void-nesters whose colonies grow large by late summer — handled at the nest entrance.

### Hornets

Large aerial nests in trees, shrubs, and structures — removed with proper protection and technique.

### Honey Bees & Africanized Bees

We treat nuisance and defensive stinging insects; for honey bee swarms we’ll advise on the safest path. South Florida bees can be Africanized, so we take extra caution.
