## Why a hive in the structure has to go

Bees in a wall void or soffit rarely resolve on their own — and the longer they stay, the bigger the job.

### Sting risk

An active colony near doors, windows, or a patio is a daily hazard — especially for kids, pets, and anyone with allergies.

### Structural & pest damage

Abandoned comb, honey, and wax left in a wall attract rodents, roaches, and moths, and can stain and rot building materials.

### It gets worse with time

Colonies grow. What starts as a small entry at a soffit becomes a larger hive that’s harder — and costlier — to reach and remove.

Straight talk: we don’t do live relocations

Bugsy’s removes and treats problem hives — we do not perform live bee removals or relocations. We take out the hive and, in most cases, repair the area we opened to get to it, so you’re left with the problem solved and the structure closed back up.
