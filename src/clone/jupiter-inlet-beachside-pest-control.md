## Why the inlet and beachside have their own pests

The Jupiter Inlet, the lighthouse district, and the beachside stretch from Jupiter Island down toward Carlin Park put homes and condos between the ocean and the inlet, wrapped in dense coastal landscaping and sea grape. It’s spectacular, and the coastal setting drives a specific set of pests.

The inlet and ocean breed mosquitoes and, especially, **no-see-ums** that swarm docks, patios, and balconies at dawn and dusk. Dense coastal landscaping and sea grape give roof rats cover to the roofline, and the warm salt-air environment keeps ants and palmetto bugs active year-round.
