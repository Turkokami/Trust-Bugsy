## Why waterfront living means different pests

Royal Palm Yacht & Country Club is a gated, deep-water community where nearly every estate has a seawall, a dock, and canal access to the Intracoastal and out to the ocean. It’s prized for exactly that — and that water-everywhere layout reshapes which pests you fight.

Seawalls, docks, and dense waterfront landscaping give roof rats continuous elevated cover right to the house, with no need to cross open ground. The canals and the Intracoastal breed mosquitoes and, especially, **no-see-ums** that come off the water at dawn and dusk and slip through standard screens onto docks and patios. Standard inland routes miss what a waterfront estate actually needs.
