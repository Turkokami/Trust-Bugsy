### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Golf-course lakes and water features are prime breeding grounds; mosquitoes and midges hit lake- and fairway-front homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

Subterranean termites attack from moist soil; iguanas colonize lake banks and seawalls, damaging landscaping.
