## Why roaches and palmetto bugs surge indoors when it rains

The big reddish-brown “palmetto bugs” South Floridians know are mostly American cockroaches, which normally live outdoors in mulch, sewers, drains, tree holes, and leaf litter. When the summer rains flood those spaces, the roaches move to higher, drier ground — and that often means your garage, kitchen, and bathrooms. German cockroaches, by contrast, live and breed indoors year-round and multiply fast if they get established.

It’s not about cleanliness — even spotless homes get palmetto bugs during the rainy season because the pressure is coming from outside. Lasting control means treating the exterior harborage and entry points, not just spraying the one you saw. A single female American cockroach and its offspring can produce hundreds of new roaches in a year, so staying ahead of them matters.

- Heavy rain floods outdoor harborage and pushes roaches indoors
- Warm, humid nights keep them active and breeding
- Drains, gaps under doors, and utility penetrations are the usual entry points
- German roaches indoors breed quickly and need a targeted interior program

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do I get palmetto bugs even though my house is clean?
Because the pressure is coming from outside. American cockroaches (“palmetto bugs”) live in mulch, drains, and landscaping, and summer rain floods those areas and drives them indoors. It has little to do with housekeeping — which is why exterior treatment and sealing matter more than cleaning alone.

### What’s the difference between palmetto bugs and German cockroaches?
“Palmetto bug” usually means the large American cockroach, which mostly lives outdoors and wanders in. German cockroaches are smaller, live and breed indoors, and multiply fast — they need a targeted interior program, while American roaches are controlled mainly from the outside in.

### When is roach season worst in Palm Beach County?
It peaks with the summer rainy season (roughly May–October) when flooding outdoor harborage pushes roaches inside, and warm humid nights keep them active. Warm winters mean some activity year-round, but the wet-season surge is the big one.

### Will one treatment get rid of them?
A treatment gives quick relief, but because the source is outdoors and the pressure is constant in summer, a recurring program is what actually keeps them out. We treat the harborage, seal entry points, and maintain a barrier through the season.

### Are cockroach treatments safe for kids and pets?
Yes. We use EPA-registered products and place baits and treatments in cracks, voids, and exterior harborage rather than open living areas, and we’ll give you clear guidance on any areas to keep clear until dry.
