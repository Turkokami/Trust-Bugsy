## Why a golf-resort community draws these pests

PGA National wraps estate homes, villas, and condos around championship courses, interconnected lakes, and manicured landscaping. It’s resort golf living, and the water and green space concentrate a specific set of pests.

The course lakes and water features breed mosquitoes all season; the open sunny turf invites fire ants and chinch bugs; the mature landscaping shelters ants and gives roof rats cover; and in the villas and condos, ghost ants and German cockroaches travel shared walls unit to unit. Slab homes still face subterranean termites from the soil.
