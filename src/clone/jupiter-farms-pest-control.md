## Why acreage by the wetlands drives the heaviest pressure

Jupiter Farms is a big rural community west toward the Loxahatchee — acreage homesteads, horse properties, and ranchettes on unpaved lanes, threaded with canals and bordered by wetland and preserve land. It’s a slice of old Florida, and it draws the heaviest, wildest pest pressure in the Jupiter area.

The canals and wetlands breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums relentlessly all wet season; the open pastures and turf invite fire ants; barns and feed rooms draw rodents; and the preserve edges push roof rats, raccoons, opossums, snakes, and other wildlife toward homes and outbuildings. Everything used around barns has to be safe for horses and livestock.
