## Why Isles at Wellington homes need coverage

The Isles at Wellington is a gated community built around a chain of lakes, with tidy, irrigated landscaping throughout. That water keeps mosquitoes and midges breeding through the wet season, and the moist landscaping feeds fast-spreading ant colonies.

With homes set close around the water, a mosquito or ant problem rarely stays on one lot. A consistent exterior plan keeps it from spreading across the neighborhood.

## The pests Isles homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Community lakes keep mosquitoes and midges breeding, hitting lakefront homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost Ants

Irrigated landscaping is perfect for fast-spreading ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors in thin lines.

### Palmetto Bugs & Roaches

American roaches move in from mulch and drains after rain; German roaches breed in kitchens once inside.

### Rodents & Termites

Rodents seek wall voids as nights cool, and subterranean termites attack from the soil under slabs.
