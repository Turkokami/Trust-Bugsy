## Why the PGA corridor drives its pests

The PGA Boulevard corridor — the Gardens Mall, Downtown at the Gardens, Legacy Place, and the surrounding offices, apartments, and shopping plazas — is the densest commercial square miles in Palm Beach Gardens. That concentration of restaurants, retail, and mixed-use structure is exactly what urban pests exploit.

German cockroaches ride shared walls, plumbing, and constant food deliveries between restaurants and units; roof rats and mice work dumpster corrals, service corridors, and plaza landscaping; and the surrounding lakes and retention ponds breed mosquitoes. A restaurant, an office, and an apartment nearby all need coordinated, discreet, documented service.
