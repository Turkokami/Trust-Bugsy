# Phorid Flies

Pest Library

## Phorid Flies

Family Phoridae (humpbacked flies)

Tiny humpbacked flies that scurry across surfaces in quick, erratic runs instead of flying — and, importantly, a possible warning sign of a broken sewer line or drain beneath a building.

## How to identify them

Phorid flies are very small and tan to dark brown, with a humped thorax that gives them a hunchbacked silhouette. Their signature behavior is telling: rather than flying off when disturbed, they dash across surfaces in quick, jerky, erratic runs before taking short flights. This scurrying habit sets them apart from fruit and drain flies.

## Behavior and biology in South Florida

Phorid flies breed in moist, decaying organic matter of many kinds — garbage, drains, rotting produce, and the organic scum in plumbing. Critically, they will also breed in the soil saturated by a broken or cracked sewer or drain line beneath a slab, which is why a persistent indoor phorid problem with no obvious surface source can be an early sign of a plumbing failure under the floor.

They are common in commercial kitchens, food-service areas, and hospitals, where hidden organic buildup and drain issues feed them.

## Signs of an infestation

- Tiny humpbacked flies running erratically across counters, walls, and floors
- Persistent flies with no obvious fruit or surface breeding source
- Activity concentrated near floor drains, base of walls, or a specific slab area
- Recurring problems in kitchens and food-service spaces

## Why they are a problem

Because phorid flies develop in filth, sewage, and decaying matter, they can carry bacteria onto food and surfaces — a real concern in kitchens and healthcare settings. Just as important, they can be the messenger for a hidden broken drain line, and ignoring them may mean overlooking a costly plumbing and sanitation problem.

## Preventing phorid flies

- Keep drains clean and free of organic film
- Address any persistent, unexplained fly activity promptly — it can signal a leak
- Fix plumbing leaks and investigate damp slab areas
- Manage garbage and clean up decaying organic matter
- In kitchens, deep-clean under and behind equipment where scum collects
