## Why the Lake Avenue core has its own pest profile

Downtown Lake Worth Beach — the Lake and Lucerne Avenue blocks of galleries, bars, restaurants, and shops, mixed with apartments and older homes — is one of the most walkable, densely built downtowns in the county, sitting just west of the Lagoon. That density plus food service and older construction is exactly what urban pests exploit.

German cockroaches ride shared walls, plumbing chases, and constant food deliveries between units and businesses; roof rats and mice work alleys, dumpster corrals, and older buildings; ants trail in from the streetscape planters; and the Lagoon a few blocks east adds mosquito pressure. A downtown restaurant and an apartment above it need coordinated, discreet, documented service.
