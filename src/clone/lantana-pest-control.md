## The pests that define Lantana

### No-See-Ums & Salt-Marsh Mosquitoes

Mangroves and tidal flats along the lagoon breed biting midges and mosquitoes that slip through screens and peak after king tides and rain.

### Ghost Ants

Salt air and moist landscaping feed fast-spreading ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baths.

### Roaches

American roaches push in from drains, mulch, and seawalls after rain; German roaches breed in kitchens once inside.

### Rodents & Termites

Roof rats travel landscaping and dock lines into attics, and subterranean termites attack from moist, sandy soil.
