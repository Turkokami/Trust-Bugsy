## Signs You Have Rodents

- Scratching, scuffling, or running sounds in the attic — especially at night
- Small black droppings on countertops, in cabinets, or along baseboards
- Chewed food packaging in the pantry or kitchen
- Greasy rub marks along walls, baseboards, or pipes
- Chewed electrical wiring — a serious fire hazard
- Nesting material (shredded insulation, paper, fabric) in the attic
- Foul odor in enclosed spaces from urine or a deceased rodent

## The Roof Rat Problem in South Florida

Roof rats are the dominant rodent species in Palm Beach and Broward counties. Unlike Norway rats (which burrow), roof rats are excellent climbers — they travel along tree branches, fences, and utility lines to reach rooflines. [UF IFAS Extension](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/) research identifies roof rats as one of the most problematic urban pests in South Florida due to the region’s mature tree canopy and warm climate.

A single pair of roof rats can produce up to 40 offspring per year. By the time most homeowners notice a rodent problem, there’s already a significant population in the attic.

## Our Complete Rodent Control Process

### Step 1: $99 Inspection (applied to the cost of treatment)

Our technician performs a full inspection of your home — the attic, roof perimeter, foundation, and all exterior walls — to identify entry points, active infestation areas, and rodent runways. We look for droppings, rub marks, chew damage, and nesting sites.

### Step 2: Professional Trapping

We strategically place snap traps along active rodent runways throughout the attic and home. Roof rat families typically range from 5 to 10 individuals. We set enough traps to eliminate the existing population as quickly as possible. Follow-up checks are included.

### Step 3: Exclusion (Rodent-Proofing)

Once the existing population is eliminated, we seal every identified entry point using materials rodents can’t chew through: galvanized steel mesh, copper mesh, hardware cloth, mortar, and expanding foam backed with metal. The most common entry areas we seal include:

- AC chase where air conditioning lines enter the home
- Plumbing pipe penetrations through the roof
- Roof vents and turbine vents
- Soffit vents along the eaves
- Gable vent screens
- Garage door threshold gaps

### Step 4: Exterior Bait Station Program (Optional)

After exclusion is complete, we recommend installing tamper-proof exterior bait stations around your home’s foundation perimeter. These stations reduce the outdoor rodent population — eliminating the pressure that drives rodents to seek entry in the first place. Bait is maintained on a monthly, bi-monthly, or quarterly schedule.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I have roof rats or mice?
Roof rats are larger (about the size of a fist), leave larger droppings, and are almost always found in attics. Mice are smaller, leave tiny droppings, and are more commonly found in kitchen and pantry areas. Both require professional treatment.

### Can rats chew through steel mesh?
No. Standard wire or plastic mesh won’t stop roof rats — they can chew through it. We use galvanized steel hardware cloth and other materials specifically rated to resist rodent gnawing.

### Do I need to replace my attic insulation after a rodent infestation?
In most cases, yes. Rodent urine and feces contaminate insulation deeply, and the material loses its R-value efficiency over time. Bugsy’s provides full attic insulation removal and replacement as part of our complete rodent remediation service.

### How long does rodent exclusion take?
Most residential exclusion jobs are completed in one visit. For larger homes or extensive damage, a second visit may be needed. We’ll give you an accurate timeline during the $99 inspection (applied to the cost of treatment).

### What if rodents come back after exclusion?
Our exclusion work is designed to be permanent. If rodents re-enter through any point we sealed, we return to address it at no additional charge within the warranty period.
