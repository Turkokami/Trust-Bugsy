## What a mosquito misting system does

A misting system is a network of discreet tubing and nozzles installed around your property — along the eaves, fence line, and landscaping. On an automatic schedule, or a tap on your phone or remote, it releases a fine, targeted mist that settles over the yard and knocks down mosquitoes and no-see-ums where they rest and breed.

The system runs when biting insects are most active — typically dawn and dusk — so your patio, pool deck, and lawn stay usable during the hours you actually want to be outside. Between cycles there’s nothing to see, store, or spray yourself.



A Bugsy’s mosquito misting system — installed discreetly and fully managed, from setup to seasonal refills.

## Why misting beats spray-only for South Florida

Traditional mosquito treatments are periodic — a technician sprays, it works for a stretch, then pressure rebuilds. In waterfront, canal, and preserve-edge yards, where mosquitoes and no-see-ums breed constantly and fly in from off your property, that gap is exactly when the biting comes back.

A misting system closes that gap with consistent, automatic treatment, so protection doesn’t fade between visits. Paired with our source-reduction and barrier treatments, it’s the most complete way to reclaim a yard that’s otherwise unusable at dusk.
