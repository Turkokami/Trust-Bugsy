## What Are the Most Common Seasonal Pests in South Florida?

South Florida’s subtropical climate means pest activity varies throughout the year, but rarely stops completely. Understanding these patterns helps us at Bugsy’s develop targeted prevention strategies for each season.

### Spring Pest Activity (March-May)

Spring brings increased moisture and warming temperatures, triggering heightened activity in:

- **Termites**: Swarming season begins, with subterranean termites particularly active
- **Ants**: Fire ants, carpenter ants, and ghost ants emerge in large numbers
- **Mosquitoes**: Breeding increases with spring rains and standing water

### Summer Peak Season (June-August)

Our hottest, most humid months see peak activity from:

- **Cockroaches**: American and German cockroaches thrive in humid conditions
- **Wasps and Bees**: Nest-building season is in full swing
- **Spiders**: Increased prey availability leads to higher spider populations
- **Mosquitoes**: Peak breeding and activity throughout the season

### Fall Transition (September-November)

As temperatures begin to moderate, pests prepare for cooler weather:

- **Rodents**: Rats and mice seek indoor shelter as outdoor conditions change
- **Stink Bugs**: Move indoors for overwintering
- **Spiders**: Mating season brings increased indoor sightings

### Winter Mild Season (December-February)

Even our “cooler” months present pest challenges:

- **Rodents**: Continue seeking warm indoor spaces
- **Cockroaches**: Remain active year-round in heated homes
- **Silverfish**: Thrive in humid indoor environments
