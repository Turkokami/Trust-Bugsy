## Why South Florida Has a Year-Round Mosquito Problem

Unlike most of the U.S., South Florida rarely gets cold enough to kill adult mosquitoes. The [Florida Department of Agriculture](https://www.fdacs.gov/Divisions-Offices/Agricultural-Environmental-Services/Bureau-of-Entomology-Nematology-Plant-Pathology/Pest-Alerts) lists South Florida as having year-round mosquito activity. Retention ponds, canals, golf course lakes, and even small amounts of standing water in plant saucers or gutters provide continuous breeding habitat. One mosquito can lay 100–200 eggs at a time — in as little as a teaspoon of standing water.

## Our Mosquito Control Treatment

### Adult Mosquito Barrier Treatment

Using a backpack fogger, our technician applies micro-encapsulated insecticide to the areas where adult mosquitoes rest: dense vegetation, under decks, along fence lines, beneath shrubs, and in shaded areas. The micro-encapsulated formula provides longer residual protection than standard sprays.

### Larvicide Treatment for Breeding Sites

We treat any standing water on your property — storm drains, bird baths, ornamental ponds, retention areas — with biological larvicide. The active ingredient (Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis, or BTi) is a naturally occurring bacteria that kills mosquito larvae without harming fish, wildlife, pets, or beneficial insects. This dual approach — adult elimination + larval prevention — delivers the most complete protection. Per [EPA guidelines](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol), biological larvicides are among the safest and most effective mosquito control tools available.

## Reducing Mosquitoes Between Treatments

- Empty and refill bird baths, pet water bowls, and plant saucers twice a week
- Keep gutters clean and free-flowing — clogged gutters are a major breeding site
- Remove or drill drainage holes in any containers that collect rainwater
- Keep swimming pool water properly treated and circulating
- Trim dense vegetation that provides shaded resting areas for adult mosquitoes
- Fix any low spots in the yard that collect standing water after rain

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is your mosquito treatment safe for my kids and pets?
Yes. We use EPA-registered products and biological larvicide that is safe for children, pets, fish, and beneficial insects like bees and butterflies. We’ll advise you to stay off treated areas until dry — usually 30–45 minutes.

### How quickly does mosquito treatment work?
Most customers notice a significant reduction in mosquito activity within 24–48 hours of treatment. The full effect builds over the first few days as the product settles into the treatment area.

### Do you treat the inside of my home for mosquitoes?
Mosquito control is primarily an outdoor service — treating the areas where mosquitoes breed and rest outside your home. If you have a significant indoor mosquito problem, there’s usually a specific entry point or breeding site we can help identify.

### Can you treat for mosquitoes before my outdoor event?
Yes. We offer one-time event treatments with 24–48 hour scheduling. Contact us as early as possible before your event date.
