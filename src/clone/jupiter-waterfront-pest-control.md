## Why waterfront living means different pests

Admirals Cove and Jupiter’s other waterfront enclaves — the estates on the golf-and-yacht canals, and the homes on the Loxahatchee River and the Intracoastal — sit against seawalls, docks, and open water. It’s the pinnacle of Jupiter living, and it reshapes which pests you fight.

Seawalls, docks, and dense waterfront landscaping give roof rats continuous elevated cover to the roofline. The river, inlet, and Intracoastal breed mosquitoes and, especially, **no-see-ums** that come off the water at dawn and dusk onto docks and terraces. Standard inland routes miss what a waterfront estate needs.
