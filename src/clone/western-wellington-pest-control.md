## Why western Wellington faces the heaviest pressure

Western Wellington — the equestrian estates, ranchettes, and family homes running west toward Loxahatchee, The Acreage, and the wetlands — sits right on the edge of the wild, threaded with canals, ponds, and preserve land. It’s the most rural, watery part of town, and it draws the heaviest pest pressure in the county.

The canals, ponds, and wetlands breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums relentlessly all wet season; the open turf and pastures invite fire ants; and the preserve edges push roof rats, raccoons, opossums, snakes, and other wildlife toward homes and barns. Big lots and slab homes still face subterranean termites from the soil.
