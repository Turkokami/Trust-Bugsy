## Why Retail Environments Are High-Risk for Pest Pressure

South Florida retail properties face pest pressure from multiple vectors: constant customer foot traffic, frequent deliveries, shared wall voids between tenants, and in many cases, food or organic materials in inventory or food court operations. Shopping centers and strip malls create connected pest corridors through utility tunnels, grease traps, and common areas — meaning one tenant’s infestation can quickly become a property-wide problem. Retailers with food operations face the additional risk of health department violations and potential temporary closures. A proactive, documented retail pest control program in Palm Beach, Broward, Martin & St. Lucie counties protects every tenant and the property’s overall reputation.

- Roof rats & mice — Enter through loading dock areas and delivery entrances; common in larger shopping centers with grocery anchors. Our
- Ghost ants & fire ants — Attracted to food sampling areas, trash receptacles, and any spilled organic material. Our
- Stored product insects — Affect grocery retailers, pet supply stores, and any tenant storing organic dry goods.
- Filth flies — Breed near dumpsters, compactors, and food waste areas — a persistent issue in shopping centers with food court operations.
- Spiders — Accumulate in stockrooms, entryways, exterior signage areas, and parking structure corners.
