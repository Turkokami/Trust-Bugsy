## Why Hotel Pest Control Requires a Specialized Program

Hotels face a pest challenge unlike any other commercial environment: continuous guest turnover creates a constant vector for new pest introductions, especially bed bugs. Shared walls, utility chases, and laundry facilities in multi-floor properties allow cockroaches, rodents, and ants to spread from one room to an entire wing if not managed systematically. Florida’s Department of Business and Professional Regulation inspects hotel kitchens and food service areas; online travel agencies (Booking.com, Expedia, TripAdvisor) increasingly monitor pest-related complaints. A documented, proactive hospitality pest control program in South Florida is essential to protecting your rating, your revenue, and your license.

Answers to Your Pest Control Questions

### How do you handle a bed bug situation without alarming guests?
We coordinate directly with your GM and housekeeping team. Affected rooms are treated using a protocol that minimizes guest visibility. Single-room treatment is typically complete within a few hours.

### How often should hotel rooms be inspected for bed bugs?
High-volume hotels benefit from quarterly inspections of all rooms, plus immediate inspection of any room following a guest complaint. We build proactive programs around your occupancy calendar.

### Can you coordinate across multiple hotel properties?
Yes. We offer multi-property programs with standardized service schedules, centralized reporting, and a single point of contact for your operations team.

### What's your response time for an urgent pest situation at a hotel?
For active commercial clients we prioritize urgent calls and can typically respond within 24 hours, with same-day service available for critical situations.
