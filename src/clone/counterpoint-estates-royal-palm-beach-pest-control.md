## Why Counterpoint Estates homes need year-round coverage

Counterpoint Estates is one of Royal Palm Beach’s established neighborhoods — larger lots, mature shade trees, and canal or lake frontage on many streets. That canopy is a highway for roof rats into attics, and the waterways breed mosquitoes and midges that drift into yards at dusk.

Settled homes also develop the small gaps — around soffits, pipe penetrations, and slab edges — that ants, roaches, and rodents exploit to get inside. We seal entry points and treat the colony at its source, not just the trail you can see.

## The pests Counterpoint Estates homes face

### Roof Rats & Wildlife

Mature oaks and ficus let roof rats travel branch-to-roof into attics and soffits; canal edges also draw opossums and the occasional raccoon.

### Ghost & Crazy Ants

Moist landscaping and canal banks feed multi-queen ant colonies that trail indoors along baseboards and counters in relentless thin lines.

### Palmetto Bugs & Roaches

American roaches push in from mulch and storm drains after rain; German roaches breed fast in kitchens once inside older homes.

### Mosquitoes & Termites

Canals and lakes breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums, while subterranean termites attack from the soil under established slabs.
