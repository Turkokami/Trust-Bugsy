## Why Hunters Run stays under steady pest pressure

Hunters Run is a large gated country-club community built around three golf courses and a network of lakes, with dense, mature landscaping and clustered villas and condos. All that water and greenery keeps mosquitoes, midges, and ants active nearly year-round.

With homes set close together, an ant or roach issue can move quickly between units. We tailor treatment to single-family homes, villas, and condos and time service to the season.

## The pests Hunters Run homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Golf-course lakes and water features are prime breeding grounds; mosquitoes and midges hit lake- and fairway-front homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Moist, maintained landscaping feeds big multi-queen colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches move in from mulch, drains, and lake edges after rain, showing up in garages and lanais.

### Roof Rats & Termites

Mature landscaping gives roof rats a route to attics; subterranean termites attack from the soil under slabs.
