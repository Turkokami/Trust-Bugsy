## Why Wellington’s established core drives its pests

The Landings and the neighborhoods around the original Wellington Town Center — near the mall, the amphitheater, and the chain of community lakes — make up the mature, established heart of Wellington, with some of the town’s oldest homes and heaviest tree cover.

The mature canopy gives roof rats a bridge to older eaves and attics; the community lakes breed mosquitoes; ghost ants trail from established landscaping into kitchens; palmetto bugs work the mulch and older plumbing; and older homes carry termite risk from original wood. An established neighborhood needs a program tuned to it, not a new-construction routine.
