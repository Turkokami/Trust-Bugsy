## Why Hollywood Beach homes need year-round coverage

Hollywood Beach packs homes, condos, and vacation rentals between the Atlantic and the Intracoastal, ringed by mangroves and the famous Broadwalk. That water is a powerhouse for no-see-ums and salt-marsh mosquitoes, which slip through screens and swarm balconies and patios at dusk.

## The pests Hollywood Beach homes face

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

The beach, Intracoastal, and mangrove edges breed biting midges and mosquitoes that swarm patios, balconies, and pool decks at dawn and dusk.

### Iguanas & Wildlife

Seawalls and canal banks are prime iguana habitat — they burrow, damage landscaping, and foul docks and pools.

### Roof Rats

Mature tropical landscaping and condo chases give roof rats routes into attics, soffits, and units.

### Palmetto Bugs & Termites

American roaches push up from sewers and drains after rain, while subterranean termites attack from the moist, sandy soil.
