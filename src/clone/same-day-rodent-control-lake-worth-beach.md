## What Are the Signs of Rodent Infestation That Require Professional Rodent Control?

Look for clear indicators that suggest professional intervention is needed.

- **Auditory Clues**: Scratching or scurrying in walls or ceilings.
- **Droppings**: Found near food, cupboards or storage.
- **Visible Entry Points**: Chewed holes or gaps in structures.

Early detection allows faster, more effective treatment.

## How to Identify Common Rodent Activity in Lake Worth Beach Homes and Businesses?

Common signs include unusual noises, droppings, gnaw marks, and nesting materials in hidden spaces.

- **Auditory Clues**: Nighttime movement and scratching.
- **Droppings & Gnaw Marks**: Evidence near food or storage.
- **Nesting Materials**: Shredded paper, fabric, or insulation used as nests.

Regular checks of attics, basements, and kitchens help spot activity early.
