## Why attic insulation matters so much in Delray Beach

South Florida runs the air conditioning nearly year-round, and your attic insulation is the barrier standing between that cool air and Delray’s relentless heat. When insulation is old, thin, or matted down by rodents, your A/C works harder and your bills climb — every single month.

It’s also a pest issue. Rodents nest in and soil attic insulation, and standard insulation offers no defense against the insects that find their way in. That’s where TAP comes in.

## What is TAP pest-control insulation?

TAP (Thermal, Acoustical, Pest-control) is a premium cellulose insulation treated with borate. It insulates and dampens sound like top-tier insulation — and because of the borate treatment, it also controls insects like ants, roaches, silverfish, and more that venture into the attic. One product, three benefits: lower energy bills, a quieter home, and built-in pest resistance.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is TAP insulation and how does it control pests?
TAP (Thermal, Acoustical, Pest-control) is borate-treated cellulose insulation. The borate is lethal to insects like ants, roaches, and silverfish that crawl through it, so the insulation itself provides ongoing pest control while it insulates and soundproofs your home.

### Will new attic insulation actually lower my energy bills?
In Delray Beach’s climate, where A/C runs most of the year, proper attic insulation is one of the highest-impact energy upgrades — old, thin, or rodent-matted insulation lets cool air escape and drives cooling costs up.

### Do I need to remove the old insulation first?
Not always — if your existing insulation is clean and simply too thin, we can top off over it. If it’s contaminated or matted by rodents, we remove and replace it so the new insulation performs and the contamination is gone.

### Can you replace insulation rodents have damaged?
Yes — that’s one of the most common reasons we’re called. We remove the soiled, matted insulation, sanitize the space, and install fresh insulation, ideally paired with exclusion so rodents can’t get back in.

### Is TAP insulation safe for my family and pets?
Yes. The borate levels that control insects are low-toxicity to people and pets, and TAP is installed in the attic and widely used in homes for exactly this combination of energy and pest benefits.

### How is this different from attic sanitation?
Sanitation is the cleanup of contamination after rodents or wildlife; insulation is restoring and upgrading the insulation itself. They often go together — sanitize the mess, then install fresh (ideally TAP) insulation.
