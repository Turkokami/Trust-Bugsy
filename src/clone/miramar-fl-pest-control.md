## Miramar Pest Control — Frequently Asked Questions

### Why am I seeing more ants after it rains in Miramar?
Rain saturates ant colonies in the ground, forcing worker ants to move above ground and into homes. Non-repellent bait treatment is the most effective solution — it works even when ants are actively foraging.

### My new Miramar home was just built. Do I need termite protection?
Yes. New construction in South Florida must have a soil pre-treatment for termites, but this protection degrades over time. We recommend a bait station monitoring program to catch any activity before it causes structural damage.

### How often should I treat for mosquitoes if I live on a lake in Miramar?
Monthly treatment is the standard recommendation for waterfront properties in South Florida. We treat both the perimeter barrier and the water’s edge with biologics safe for fish and wildlife.
