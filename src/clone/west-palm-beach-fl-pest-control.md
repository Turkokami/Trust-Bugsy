## Ant Control in West Palm Beach

Ghost ants — often called sugar ants — are the most commonly reported pest in West Palm Beach homes. They form large, fast-moving trails along baseboards and countertops, especially after rain. Most over-the-counter sprays make the problem worse by causing colonies to split. Bugsy’s uses non-repellent baiting that worker ants carry back to the colony, eliminating the entire nest including the queen. For fire ants in your lawn, we apply broadcast granular bait that targets the whole property. Learn more about our [ant control](/ant-control/) service.

## Termite Control in West Palm Beach

West Palm Beach’s subtropical climate supports both drywood and subterranean termite populations. Subterranean termites travel underground up to 150 feet searching for wood, building mud tubes on foundation walls. Drywood termites infest door frames, window sills, and attic lumber — often going unnoticed until serious damage has occurred. According to the [EPA termite identification guide](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol/termites-how-identify-and-control-them), early detection is critical. Bugsy’s offers no-tent localized treatments for smaller infestations, and full tent fumigation for severe cases, all backed by a written warranty. See our [termite control](/termite-control/) page for full details.

## Rodent Control in West Palm Beach

Roof rats thrive in West Palm Beach’s mature tree canopy, traveling along branches to access rooflines and attics. According to [CDC](), rodents spread disease and can cause significant property damage. Signs include scratching in the attic at night, droppings near cabinets, and chewed wiring. Bugsy’s provides full rodent control: inspection, professional trapping, entry-point exclusion, and when contamination is present, full [attic insulation removal and replacement](/attic-sanitation-insulation-removal/).
