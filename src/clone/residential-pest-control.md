## Our 3-Step Process

### 1. Thorough Inspection

Our technician inspects every room, the attic, garage, and the full exterior perimeter. We look for active pest activity, entry points, moisture issues, and conditions that attract pests — not just the problems you already know about.

### 2. Customized Treatment

We don’t spray and leave. Every treatment is customized to what we find. Per [EPA Safe Pest Control guidelines](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol), targeted application of the right products in the right locations is far more effective — and safer — than blanket spraying.

### 3. Ongoing Prevention

After the initial cleanout, most customers switch to exterior-only quarterly service — you don’t even need to be home. If any pests return indoors between visits, we come back for free.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I get pest control in South Florida?
Most South Florida homes benefit from quarterly service at minimum. Because our climate has no true winter, monthly or bi-monthly plans provide the strongest year-round protection and are often the most cost-effective option long-term.

### Is Bugsy’s pest control safe for my kids and pets?
Yes. We use EPA-registered, eco-friendly products specifically selected for safety around families and pets. We’ll always advise you of any post-treatment precautions for your specific home.

### Do I need to be home for pest control service?
For the initial interior treatment, yes — we’ll need to treat inside. After that, most customers move to exterior-only quarterly service, which means you don’t need to be home at all.

### What if pests come back after treatment?
Every Bugsy’s service plan includes our pest-free guarantee. If pests return between your scheduled visits, call us and we’ll return for a free retreatment — no questions asked.
