## Why Eagle Trace homes need year-round coverage

Eagle Trace is a gated golf community in Coral Springs — a former tournament course wrapped in lakes and mature landscaping near the western edge of Broward. Those water features and the nearby Everglades keep mosquitoes and midges active nearly year-round, and lakefront and fairway homes feel it first.

## The pests Eagle Trace homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Community lakes, canals, and the nearby Everglades edge breed mosquitoes and biting midges that swarm patios and pool decks at dawn and dusk.

### Fire Ants & Ghost Ants

Open turf and irrigated landscaping feed fire-ant mounds and ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches push up from sewers and drains after rain, turning up in garages, lanais, and kitchens.

### Iguanas & Roof Rats

Iguanas colonize lake banks and canals, while roof rats travel the mature canopy into attics and soffits.
