## Why College Park’s setting drives its pests

College Park wraps 1920s and mid-century bungalows around the Lake Worth Beach municipal golf course, a few blocks off the Lagoon, under a mature tree canopy. It’s a tight-knit, walkable neighborhood, and that green, established character is part of the pest equation.

Older homes bring termite risk from original wood; the overlapping canopy bridges roof rats to older eaves and attics; ghost and white-footed ants trail from mature landscaping into kitchens; palmetto bugs work the mulch and older plumbing; and the golf-course lakes and Lagoon add mosquito pressure to shaded yards.
