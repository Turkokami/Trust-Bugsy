## Why pests love downtown Boca’s density

Downtown Boca — the blocks around Mizner Park, Royal Palm Place, and the Federal Highway corridor — is one of the most tightly-built, walkable pockets in the city. High-rise condos, lofts, restaurants, and offices share walls, plumbing, and trash areas, and that density is exactly what pests exploit.

When homes and food businesses sit this close, a pest problem is rarely one address. German cockroaches ride in on deliveries and travel shared walls and plumbing; rodents work the alleys and older mixed-use buildings; and ants trail in from the landscaped courtyards and planters. Treating a downtown unit in isolation is why store-bought fixes never hold here.

## The pests we see most downtown

Density-driven pressure where homes and food businesses meet.

### German Cockroaches

The signature pest of dense, food-adjacent buildings — they spread through shared walls and plumbing. Our gel-and-monitor program breaks the cycle.

### Roof Rats & Mice

### Ants

Ghost and white-footed ants trail from courtyards and planters into kitchens and break rooms — treated at the trail and the nest.

### Palmetto Bugs
