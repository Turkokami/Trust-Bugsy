## The pests Paddock Park homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Wellington’s canals and wet turf breed mosquitoes and midges that swarm yards at dawn and dusk.

### Fire Ants & Ghost Ants

Open turf feeds fire-ant mounds, while ghost ants trail from the landscaping into kitchens and baths.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches push in from mulch, drains, and canal edges after every heavy rain.

### Rodents & Termites

Roof rats seek attics and any feed or shed storage; subterranean termites attack from the soil.
