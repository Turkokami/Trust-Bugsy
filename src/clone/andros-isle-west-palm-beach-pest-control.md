Andros Isle is a gated community threaded with lakes and retention ponds off Palm Beach Lakes Boulevard in central West Palm Beach. The water keeps mosquitoes and midges breeding through the long wet season, and the irrigated landscaping feeds fast-spreading ant colonies.

## The pests Andros Isle homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Community lakes and retention ponds keep mosquitoes and midges breeding, hitting waterfront homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Ghost Ants

Irrigated landscaping is perfect for fast-spreading ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Palmetto Bugs & Roaches

American roaches move in from drains and mulch after rain; German roaches breed in kitchens once inside.

### Rodents & Termites

Rodents seek wall voids as nights cool, and subterranean termites attack from the soil under slabs.
