# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: January 1, 2026**

Welcome to Trust Bugsy! Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website [https://trustbugsy.com](/) and our services.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you visit our site, fill out forms, or contact us directly, we may collect the following types of information:

- **Personal Information**: Name, phone number, email address, physical address, and any other details you voluntarily provide.
- **Service Information**: Information regarding your pest control needs, service requests, and property details.
- **Automatically Collected Information**: Device information, browser type, IP address, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps collected through cookies and other tracking technologies.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Respond to inquiries and provide pest control services.
- Process service requests and send related communications.
- Improve our website and services based on your feedback.
- Send promotional materials, special offers, and updates (only if you opt-in).

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others.
We may share information with trusted partners who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you — as long as those parties agree to keep your information confidential.

## 4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience. You can choose to set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that some parts of the website may not function properly without cookies.

## 5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any linked sites you visit.

## 6. Protecting Your Information

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices, and implement security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You may opt out of receiving marketing communications from us by following the unsubscribe instructions included in each email or by contacting us directly.
You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Trust Bugsy may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective Date” at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back periodically to stay informed.
