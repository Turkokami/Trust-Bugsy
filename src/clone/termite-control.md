## Types of Termites in South Florida

### Drywood Termites

Drywood termites live entirely inside the wood they’re consuming. They don’t need soil contact, which means they can infest door frames, window sills, attic lumber, baseboards, and even furniture. The first sign is often small piles of pellet-like droppings (frass) near wood, or swarming winged termites from April through July.

### Subterranean Termites

Subterranean termites live in the soil and are the most destructive species in Florida. They build mud tubes on foundation walls and can travel up to 150 feet underground searching for wood. Colonies can contain up to 1 million workers. Per [UF IFAS Extension](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/) research, subterranean termites cause the majority of termite-related structural damage in Florida.

### Formosan Termites

Formosan termites are an invasive subterranean species found in Palm Beach and Broward counties. They’re often called ‘super termites’ because they form aerial colonies inside walls and damage structures faster than native species. They require specialized treatment combining soil treatment and fumigation.

## Our Termite Control Methods

### No-Tent Termite Treatment

For localized drywood termite infestations, our [no-tent termite treatment](/no-tent-termite-treatment/) injects termiticide foam directly into the galleries where termites are active. The product flows through the colony, termites share it with nestmates, and the entire colony — including the queen — is eliminated. No tenting, no prep, no days away from home. Warranted for one year.

### Tent Fumigation

For whole-home drywood termite infestations or severe cases, [tent fumigation](/termite-control/) remains the most thorough method of elimination. The structure is sealed and filled with fumigant gas that penetrates every piece of wood in the home. Bugsy’s does not perform fumigation. If our inspection shows your home needs tenting, we’ll tell you plainly and refer you to a licensed fumigation company — and for localized drywood activity, our [no-tent termite treatment](/no-tent-termite-treatment/) is the alternative.

### Termite Bait Stations

For subterranean termite prevention, we install bait stations around your home’s perimeter at regular intervals. Worker termites find the bait, consume it, and share it throughout the colony — eliminating the entire underground population. Annual monitoring visits are included to inspect and replenish bait as needed.

### Liquid Termiticide Barrier (Trenching)

We apply a liquid termiticide directly to the soil surrounding your foundation, creating a continuous chemical barrier. Termites contacting the barrier are eliminated and transfer the product to nestmates — protecting your home for up to five years per application.

### Termite Pre-Treatments (New Construction)

Florida law requires termite pre-treatment of new construction before the foundation is poured. Bugsy’s provides same-day pre-treatments with a blue dye additive for building inspector verification. We also treat additions, remodels, and commercial new construction.

If you’re buying or selling a home in South Florida, a WDO (Wood-Destroying Organism) inspection is strongly recommended — and often required by lenders. Our licensed inspectors provide a complete visual inspection of the interior and exterior, including the attic, and deliver a formal WDO report. $99 termite inspections are available for homeowners concerned about existing activity.

## Warning Signs of Termites

- Mud tubes on foundation walls or exposed wood (subterranean)
- Small piles of pellet-like droppings (frass) near wood trim or windowsills (drywood)
- Discarded wings near windows, doors, or sinks after a swarm
- Wood that sounds hollow when tapped
- Bubbling or uneven paint on walls (can indicate moisture from termite damage)
- Visible termite swarms (flying termites) in spring
