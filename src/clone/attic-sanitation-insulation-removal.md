## Why Attic Cleanup Matters After a Rodent Infestation

- Rodent urine and droppings can remain hazardous for years if not properly removed
- Contaminated insulation loses its R-value effectiveness, increasing energy bills
- The odor from rodent waste attracts new rodents — creating a re-infestation cycle
- Nesting material and droppings promote mold growth in enclosed attic spaces
- Chewed electrical wiring in contaminated insulation is a fire hazard

## Our Attic Restoration Process

### 1. Full Attic Inspection

Before any remediation begins, we perform a complete inspection of the attic to document the extent of contamination, identify any remaining rodent entry points, and assess the condition of existing insulation, wiring, and ducts.

### 2. Contaminated Insulation Removal

Using commercial-grade HEPA-rated vacuum equipment and full personal protective equipment, our team removes all contaminated insulation from the attic floor and any accessible cavity areas. Contaminated material is bagged and disposed of according to [EPA guidelines](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol) for biological waste.

### 3. Attic Disinfection & Fogging

After insulation is removed, we fog the entire attic space with a hospital-grade disinfectant effective against bacteria, viruses, and fungal spores. The fogging reaches all surfaces — rafters, sheeting, joists — ensuring no pathogens remain.

### 4. New Insulation Installation

We install fresh blown-in insulation to restore your attic’s thermal efficiency. New insulation is installed to the appropriate R-value for South Florida (R-30 to R-38 is typically recommended). View our [insulation replacement service](https://trustbugsy.com/service/insulation-replacement/) for full details on insulation options.

### 5. Final Inspection & Entry Point Verification

After installation, we perform a final walkthrough to confirm all entry points are sealed (as part of our [rodent control](https://trustbugsy.com/service/rat-control-extermination/) exclusion service) and that the attic is clean, sealed, and properly insulated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my attic insulation needs to be replaced?
If you’ve had an active rodent infestation, we recommend a free attic inspection. Signs include visible droppings in the insulation, compressed or displaced insulation from nesting, odor in the attic or upper floors, and evidence of chewed wiring or material.

### Is attic sanitation covered by homeowners insurance?
In some cases, yes. Rodent damage and contamination cleanup may be covered under your homeowners policy depending on your coverage. We can provide detailed documentation of damage and work performed for insurance claims.

### How long does attic insulation removal take?
Most standard residential attic insulation removal and replacement is completed in one day. Larger homes or more extensive contamination may require two visits.

### Do I need to be home during attic sanitation?
It’s helpful to have someone available at the start and end of the job, but we can work independently once access is arranged. We’ll coordinate timing with you at scheduling.
