## Why South Florida Office Buildings Need Year-Round Commercial Pest Control

South Florida’s climate means pest pressure doesn’t slow down between seasons. Ghost ants and white-footed ants invade break rooms and kitchenettes year-round. German cockroaches exploit the plumbing in shared restrooms and server rooms. Roof rats and mice follow HVAC lines and utility conduits through multi-story buildings. In multi-tenant properties, one unit’s pest issue can quickly spread through shared wall voids and corridors to neighboring suites. A documented commercial pest control program protects tenants, reduces liability, and maintains the professional appearance every office building must deliver to retain occupants.

Answers to Your Pest Control Questions

### Will pest control disrupt our workday or alarm tenants?
No. We schedule service during evenings, weekends, or early mornings and use discreet, odor-free treatments. Tenants will not be aware a service occurred.

### We have a multi-tenant building. Can you cover all suites?
Yes. We coordinate building-wide programs with individual suite scheduling and provide consolidated service summaries to property management.

### How do you handle cockroaches in a shared restroom?
We use gel bait applications and drain treatment in harborage areas — applied after hours with no visible residue. We address the root cause rather than just treating visible insects.

### Do you offer bed bug inspections for office environments?
Yes. If bed bugs are suspected, we provide a professional inspection and a targeted treatment plan. Early response is critical to preventing spread between suites.
