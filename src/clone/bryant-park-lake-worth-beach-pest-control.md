## Why Bryant Park homes need coverage

Bryant Park sits right on the Lake Worth Lagoon, a walkable historic district of older homes beside the waterfront park and marina. The lagoon and mangroves breed no-see-ums and mosquitoes, and the mature canopy is a route for roof rats into attics.

Older, settled homes develop the gaps that ants, roaches, and rodents exploit. We seal entry points and target the source, timing exterior service to the tides and wet season.

## The pests Bryant Park homes face

### Roof Rats & Rodents

Mature oaks and ficus put roof rats onto rooftops and into attics; settled bungalows offer plenty of gaps to exploit.

### Drywood & Subterranean Termites

Original wood framing and trim attract drywood termites, while subterranean termites attack from the soil.

### Ghost Ants

Older slabs and moist landscaping feed ghost-ant colonies that trail into kitchens and bathrooms.

### No-See-Ums & Palmetto Bugs

The Intracoastal and lagoon breed biting midges; American roaches push in from drains and mulch after rain.
