Property managers who consolidate commercial pest control under one reliable partner gain consistency, simplified billing, and a vendor who knows their entire portfolio.

Whether you manage 5 properties or 50, we build a South Florida property management pest control program that scales with your portfolio, standardizes your reporting, and resolves tenant complaints fast.

## Why Property Managers Need a Dedicated Pest Control Partner

Coordinating pest control across multiple properties with multiple vendors creates inconsistency, administrative overhead, and accountability gaps. Tenant pest complaints are among the top reasons residents choose not to renew leases — and Florida landlord-tenant law places clear obligations on property owners to maintain habitable conditions, which explicitly includes pest management. A single, accountable pest control partner who understands your portfolio, responds quickly to tenant complaints, and provides standardized documentation eliminates the friction and liability that come with a fragmented vendor approach. We serve commercial, residential, and mixed-use portfolios across Palm Beach County, Broward County, and St. Lucie County.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you handle multiple property types — residential, commercial, mixed-use?
Yes. We service residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties and tailor each program to that property type while maintaining consistent reporting and billing across your portfolio.

### How quickly can you respond to a tenant pest complaint?
For property management clients we prioritize complaint-driven calls and typically respond within 24-48 hours. For urgent situations like bed bugs we aim for same-day or next-day service.

### What does a tenant turnover pest control service include?
We inspect the vacated unit for pest activity, treat any findings, and provide a written certification that the unit has been inspected and treated — giving your new tenant a pest-free start and protecting you from day-one complaints.

### Do you provide documentation we can share with property owners?
Yes. We provide service reports after every visit that can be shared with property owners for their records, plus a full service history per property available on request.
