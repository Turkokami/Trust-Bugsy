## Why a master-planned community draws these pests

Abacoa packs townhome villages, single-family neighborhoods, apartments, and the walkable town center around Roger Dean Stadium and preserve greenways. It’s designed for community living, and the density plus green space concentrates a specific set of pests.

In the townhome villages, ghost ants and German cockroaches travel shared walls and plumbing unit to unit; the greenways and preserve buffers breed mosquitoes and give roof rats cover; and the town center’s restaurants add their own pressure. Shared-wall living means treating one home in isolation rarely holds — building-aware, HOA-coordinated service is what works.
