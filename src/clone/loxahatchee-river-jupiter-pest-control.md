## Why the river and preserves drive these pests

The Loxahatchee River — a federally designated Wild and Scenic river — and the neighborhoods along it and around Riverbend Park put homes right against tidal water, cypress swamp, and old-growth preserve. It’s beautiful, and that river-and-preserve setting is a powerful pest driver.

The river and its tidal creeks breed mosquitoes and, especially, **no-see-ums** off the water at dawn and dusk. The cypress preserves and dense riverbank landscaping give roof rats cover to the roofline and push raccoons, opossums, and other wildlife toward homes. Riverfront homes need a program that treats the water and preserve edge, not just the house.
