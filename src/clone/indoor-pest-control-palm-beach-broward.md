### Signs of Pest Infestation in South Florida Homes

Our technicians train homeowners to recognize early warning signs:

- Ant trails along baseboards or near food sources
- Dark droppings in cabinets, drawers, or behind appliances
- Gnaw marks on food packaging or structural materials
- Unusual odors, especially musty smells in humid areas
- Live or dead insects near windows, doors, or light fixtures
- Sounds in walls or ceilings, particularly at night

Early detection allows for more effective, less invasive treatments.

## Effective Tips for Preventing Indoor Pest Infestations in South Florida

Prevention is always more cost-effective than treatment. Our technicians share these proven strategies:

### Home Maintenance for Pest Prevention

**Moisture Control**: Use dehumidifiers and fix leaks promptly—Florida’s humidity attracts many pests

**Seal Entry Points**: Caulk cracks around windows, doors, and utility penetrations

**Landscape Management**: Keep vegetation trimmed away from your home’s exterior

**Proper Drainage**: Ensure gutters and drainage systems direct water away from your foundation

### Daily Habits That Deter Pests

- Store food in airtight containers, especially cereals and pet food
- Clean kitchen surfaces and sweep floors regularly
- Take out garbage frequently and use tight-fitting lids
- Fix leaky faucets and eliminate standing water
- Keep counters dry and wipe up spills immediately

### When to Schedule Regular Inspections

In South Florida’s year-round pest-friendly climate, we recommend:

- **Quarterly inspections** for most homes
- **Bi-monthly service** for homes with previous infestations
- **Seasonal treatments** to address specific pest cycles
- **Post-storm inspections** after hurricanes or severe weather
