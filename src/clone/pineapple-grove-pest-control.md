## Why pests love Pineapple Grove’s density

Pineapple Grove — the Arts District running up NE 2nd Avenue from Atlantic toward the tennis center — is one of the most walkable, tightly-built pockets in Delray Beach. Renovated warehouse lofts, new townhomes, galleries, breweries, coffee shops, and restaurants sit shoulder to shoulder, often sharing walls, alleys, and dumpster areas. That density is exactly what pests exploit.

When homes and food businesses are this close together, a pest problem is rarely just one address. German cockroaches ride in on deliveries and travel shared plumbing and party walls; rodents work the alleys and older converted-warehouse foundations; and ants trail in from the landscaped sidewalks and planters that make the Grove so pleasant. Treating a Pineapple Grove unit in isolation without accounting for the block around it is why store-bought fixes never hold here.
