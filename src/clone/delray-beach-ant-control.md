## Why ants never seem to quit in Delray Beach

Delray Beach’s warmth, humidity, and lush landscaping give ants everything they need to build enormous, multi-queen colonies that forage indoors all year. The frustrating part for homeowners is that the ants you see on the counter are a tiny fraction of the colony — and hitting that trail with a store-bought spray often makes things worse.

Many of our worst local ants (white-footed ants, ghost ants, Caribbean crazy ants) have multiple queens and satellite nests. A repellent spray doesn’t kill the colony — it stresses it into **budding**, splitting into several new colonies. That’s why real ant control in Delray starts with identifying the exact species and treating the colony at its source.

## The ants we treat in Delray Beach

Correct ID matters — different species need completely different baits and methods.

### Ghost Ants

Tiny, pale-legged ants that trail across kitchens and bathrooms by the thousands — one of the most common Delray calls.

### White-Footed Ants

Black ants with pale feet that build million-strong colonies; they feed on plant honeydew, so baiting alone often fails.

### Big-Headed Ants

Soil-nesting ants that push up small dirt piles along driveways and foundations and trail indoors for food.

### Carpenter Ants

Large ants that nest in moisture-damaged wood — a sign of a possible hidden leak worth addressing.

### Fire Ants

Aggressive stinging ants that build mounds in Delray lawns — a real hazard for kids, pets, and bare feet.

### Crazy & Pharaoh Ants

Fast-moving crazy ants and indoor pharaoh ants that must be baited, never sprayed, or they scatter and multiply.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do the ants keep coming back no matter what I do?

Most Delray ants have multiple queens and hidden satellite nests. Killing the visible trail does nothing to the colony — and repellent sprays can make it split and spread. Lasting control means treating the colony at its source, which is what we do.

### Do over-the-counter ant sprays work?

Usually not for our local species — and they can backfire by causing colonies to bud into several new ones. Baiting and non-repellent products applied by a pro are far more effective.

### What are the tiny ants all over my kitchen?

In Delray Beach, tiny kitchen-and-bath ants are most often ghost ants or white-footed ants. They’re a nuisance, not a health threat, but they require species-specific baiting to eliminate.

### Can you treat fire ants in my yard?

Yes. We treat fire-ant mounds and can put your lawn on a program to keep them from re-establishing — important where kids and pets are barefoot.

### Are your ant treatments safe for my pets and kids?

Yes. We use pet-safe methods, target harborage and entry points rather than blanketing living space, and place baits where children and pets can’t reach them.

### Do carpenter ants mean I have a bigger problem?

Often yes — carpenter ants nest in wood softened by moisture, so finding them can point to a hidden leak or damaged wood worth correcting. We’ll flag it if we see it.
