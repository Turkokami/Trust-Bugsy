## Why Grandview Heights homes need year-round coverage

Grandview Heights is one of West Palm Beach’s oldest neighborhoods — 1920s bungalows and cottages on tight, tree-lined streets just off downtown and the Intracoastal. That charm comes with challenges: mature canopy is a highway for roof rats, and decades-old wood is a target for drywood termites.

Settled foundations and original construction develop the small gaps ants, roaches, and rodents use to get inside. We seal entry points and treat the source while respecting the character of a historic home.

## The pests historic Grandview Heights homes face

### Roof Rats & Rodents

Mature oaks and ficus put roof rats onto rooftops and into attics; settled bungalows offer plenty of gaps to exploit.

### Drywood & Subterranean Termites

Original wood framing and trim are prime targets for drywood termites, while subterranean termites attack from the soil.

### Ghost Ants

Older slabs and moist landscaping feed ghost-ant colonies that trail into kitchens and bathrooms.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches push in from drains, mulch, and the Intracoastal-area moisture, especially after rain.
