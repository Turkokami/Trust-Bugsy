# Wildlife Trapping & Exclusion

Wildlife Trapping & Exclusion

## Get them out — and seal them out for good

Rats, bats, raccoons, possums, moles, birds, and other uninvited wildlife don’t just leave on their own. Bugsy’s traps and removes the animals, then seals the exterior entry points they used to get in — because trapping without exclusion just leaves the door open for the next one.

## What we handle

One team for the whole problem — from the animal in your attic to the hole it climbed through and the mess it left behind.

### Wildlife trapping & removal

Targeted trapping and removal of nuisance wildlife — bats, raccoons, possums, moles, birds, and more — with scheduled trap checks until the property is clear.

### Rodent trapping & exclusion

Rat and mouse trapping paired with proofing of exterior entry points, six trap checks, and a warranty — the difference between “gone for now” and “gone for good.”

### Exclusion & entry sealing

We seal the potential exterior openings animals use to get in. (Exterior points only — we don’t seal interior locations.)

### Dead animal removal

Removal of dead animals from attics, crawlspaces, yards, and driveways — clearing the source of the odor.

### Bait station installation

Tamper-resistant exterior bait stations to knock down and monitor rodent pressure around the structure.

### Rodent abatement letters

Documentation of trapping activity and a formal abatement letter once activity is confirmed resolved — useful for real-estate and property records.

Why exclusion matters

## Trapping is temporary. Exclusion is the fix.

Remove an animal without sealing the way it got in, and you’re just waiting for the next one. Our rodent trapping & exclusion service proofs the potential exterior entry points, so the problem is solved at the structure — not just the symptom. It’s backed by a warranty you can keep extending as long as you maintain service.
