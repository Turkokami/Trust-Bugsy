## Why Formosan termites are different

We treat all three subterranean species active in South Florida — Formosan, Asian, and Eastern (native) subterranean termites. Formosans are the most aggressive of the group.

### Massive colonies

Formosan colonies grow far larger and more aggressive than native subterranean termites, so damage accumulates faster once they’re inside.

### They nest above ground, too

Unlike most subterranean species, Formosans can build “carton” nests inside walls and voids — sustaining an infestation even without constant soil contact.

### They eat more than wood

Beyond structural wood, they’ll damage paper, cardboard, and even bore through insulation and building materials as they forage.

## Know the warning signs

Caught early, treatment is simpler and cheaper. Call for an inspection if you notice:

- Mud foraging tubes on foundations or walls
- Swarmers or discarded wings near lights/windows
- Hollow or blistered wood
- Soft, sagging, or damaged trim
- Frass or mud in wall voids
- Bubbling paint
