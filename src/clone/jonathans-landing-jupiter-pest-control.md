## Why Jonathan’s Landing homes need coverage

Jonathan’s Landing wraps gated homes and condos around canals, a marina, and golf, all set in mature tropical landscaping near the Intracoastal. The water breeds no-see-ums and mosquitoes, and the dense planting is a route for roof rats into attics and soffits.

Seawalls and canal banks also draw iguanas, while ghost ants and termites work the moist, irrigated grounds. We treat the home, landscaping, and water edges together for lasting relief.

## The pests Jonathan’s Landing homes face

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

Canals, the Intracoastal, and mangrove edges breed biting midges and mosquitoes that swarm docks, patios, and pool decks at dusk.

### Roof Rats

Dense tropical landscaping and mature canopy let roof rats travel into attics, soffits, and outdoor kitchens.

### Iguanas & Wildlife

Iguanas colonize seawalls and canal banks, and the water draws the occasional raccoon or opossum.

### Ghost Ants & Termites

Lush irrigation feeds ghost-ant colonies; subterranean termites attack from moist, sandy soil.
