SoSo — South of Southern Boulevard, running from Olive east toward Flagler Drive and the Intracoastal — is a tight-knit neighborhood of mid-century ranches and older cottages under a mature tree canopy, popular with young families a few blocks off the water. That green, established character is part of the pest equation.

The overlapping canopy gives roof rats a path to older eaves and attics; ghost and white-footed ants trail from mature landscaping into kitchens; palmetto bugs work the mulch and older plumbing; and the Intracoastal a few blocks east adds mosquito and no-see-um pressure to shaded yards.
