## Why your attic matters

After a rodent problem — or after 15+ years of settling and moisture — attic insulation stops doing its job and starts causing problems.

### Decontamination & sanitation

Rodents leave droppings, urine, and odor in insulation. We fog and sanitize the attic to clear contamination and knock down odor.

### Old insulation removal

Full removal and haul-away of soiled, matted, or rodent-damaged insulation and debris, prepping a clean deck for a fresh install.

### TAP pest-control insulation

Borate-treated blow-in insulation that insulates, dampens sound, and resists common insects — our recommended upgrade.

### Fiberglass blow-in

Traditional loose-fill fiberglass installed to your target R-value for reliable, cost-effective coverage.

### Insulation top-off

Already have a decent base? We add depth over existing insulation to restore performance without a full removal.

### Spray foam

Spray-foam options for targeted air-sealing and higher-performance applications (scheduled after assessment).

The TAP advantage

## Insulation that fights pests

TAP (Thermal, Acoustical, Pest-control) insulation is treated with borates — the same class of mineral used in pest control. It insulates and quiets your home like premium insulation, while making the attic hostile to roaches, ants, silverfish, and other common invaders. For a pest-control company, it’s the obvious re-insulation choice.

- Energy savings
- Sound dampening
- Pest-resistant borate treatment
- Made from recycled material
