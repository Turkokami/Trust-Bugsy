## What drywood termites do

Unlike subterranean termites, drywood termites live inside the wood itself — no soil contact and no moisture required — which is why they turn up in attics, framing, trim, and even furniture.

### They live in the wood

Drywood colonies nest directly inside dry, sound structural wood — rafters, framing, window and door trim, and hardwood furniture.

### Telltale frass

They push distinctive pellet-shaped droppings (frass) out of tiny “kick-out” holes — small piles of what looks like coarse sand or sawdust.

### Localized, but spreading

Colonies are smaller than subterranean ones, but left alone they establish in multiple spots throughout a structure over time.
