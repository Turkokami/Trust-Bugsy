## Why Jupiter Country Club homes need coverage

Jupiter Country Club is a gated golf community in western Jupiter, built around lakes and bordered by preserves and open land. Those lakes and wetlands breed mosquitoes and no-see-ums, and the preserve edges bring fire ants, rodents, and wildlife toward the homes.

Homes on the water, a fairway, or the preserve edge feel it first. We tailor the treatment to your lot and the season rather than running a one-size routine.

## The pests Jupiter Country Club homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Golf lakes and nearby preserves breed biting insects that hit lake- and preserve-edge homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Fire Ants & Yard Ants

Manicured turf and preserve edges are ideal for fire-ant mounds and nuisance ants pushing toward the home.

### Rodents & Wildlife

Roof rats travel the canopy into attics, and preserve edges bring the occasional raccoon, opossum, or iguana.

### Palmetto Bugs & Termites

American roaches move in from mulch after rain; subterranean termites attack from moist soil under slabs.
