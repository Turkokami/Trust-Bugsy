## Signs of a Bed Bug Infestation

- Small rust-colored stains on mattress seams, sheets, or box springs (crushed bed bugs or fecal matter)
- Tiny white eggs or translucent shed skins in mattress seams or furniture joints
- Clusters of small, itchy red bites — often in a line or zigzag pattern on arms, legs, or neck
- A sweet, musty odor in severe infestations
- Live bed bugs — flat, oval, about the size of an apple seed — hiding in mattress seams, box spring corners, or behind headboards

## How Bed Bugs Spread

Per the [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol), bed bugs don’t jump or fly — they hitchhike. They spread through luggage, used furniture, clothing, and from unit to unit in multi-family buildings. Hotels, Airbnbs, college dormitories, hospitals, and moving trucks are common sources. In South Florida’s active hospitality and tourism market, bed bug exposure risk is higher than average.

## Our Bed Bug Treatment Process

### Thorough Inspection

Our technician performs a detailed inspection of all sleeping areas, furniture, baseboards, electrical outlets, and luggage storage areas. We identify the extent of the infestation and any secondary spread to adjacent rooms.

### Professional Chemical Treatment

We apply a combination of residual insecticides, contact-kill products, and insect growth regulators specifically labeled for bed bug control. ### Follow-Up Treatment

Bed bug eggs can survive initial treatments — a follow-up visit 2 weeks after the first treatment targets newly hatched nymphs before they reach reproductive maturity. This two-visit protocol is industry standard and essential for complete elimination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How did bed bugs get into my home?
Bed bugs are expert hitchhikers. The most common sources are recent travel (hotel stays), secondhand furniture or mattresses, and guests who brought them unknowingly. In apartments, they can also migrate between units.

### Can I treat bed bugs myself?
Consumer bed bug products have very limited effectiveness against established infestations. Bed bugs have developed resistance to many common pesticides, and their ability to hide in tiny cracks makes complete elimination without professional treatment extremely difficult.

### Do I need to throw away my mattress?
Not usually. Professional treatment of the mattress, box spring, and bed frame is typically sufficient. We’ll advise you during the inspection if any items are beyond treatment.

### How long does bed bug treatment take?
The initial treatment typically takes 2–4 hours for a standard bedroom. A follow-up treatment 2 weeks later completes the process. We’ll provide a detailed preparation checklist to make the process as smooth as possible.
