## Why fleas and ticks never take a break in Delray Beach

Fleas and ticks need warmth and humidity to thrive — and Delray Beach supplies both year-round. Without a hard freeze to reset the population, they breed continuously, which is why South Florida pet owners battle them long after “flea season” would end up north.

It’s not just your pets. Delray’s wildlife — raccoons, opossums, stray and feral cats — travels through yards and drops flea eggs and ticks as it goes, re-seeding your property even if your own pets are treated. And the flea you see is only about 5% of the problem: the eggs, larvae, and pupae hidden in carpet, bedding, and yard shade are the other 95%, which is why treating only the adults never works.

### Do I really need flea control year-round in Delray Beach?
Often, yes. Without a winter freeze, fleas and ticks stay active all year here, and wildlife constantly re-seeds yards — so ongoing prevention makes a real difference for pet households.

### Do you treat the yard too, or just inside?
Both. Fleas and ticks develop in shaded, protected yard harborage as well as indoors, so treating only inside leaves the outdoor source intact. Our program covers both.

### Do my pets need to be treated too?
Yes — environmental treatment from us plus on-pet treatment from your veterinarian is the combination that actually breaks a flea infestation. We coordinate the timing with you.

### How long does it take to get rid of fleas?
Because of the hidden pupae, most flea jobs take an initial treatment plus a follow-up over a few weeks to fully break the cycle — we’ll set the schedule at your inspection.

### Are the treatments safe around my pets and kids?
Yes. We use products and methods designed for pet households, apply them appropriately, and give you simple, brief precautions (like keeping off treated areas until dry).

### Can you help with ticks specifically?
Yes. We reduce tick harborage in the yard — tall grass, leaf litter, and shaded edges — and treat the areas where ticks wait for a host, especially important for homes near natural areas and with wildlife traffic.
