## Why the Estate Section demands a proactive, discreet program

The Estate Section — the south-island district of grand oceanfront and lakefront mansions — combines the heaviest biting-insect pressure on the island with the densest tropical landscaping. Water on both sides breeds no-see-ums and mosquitoes, while sprawling ficus hedges and gardens shelter roof rats and ants right against the home.

These historic and architecturally significant estates also face subterranean and drywood termites in fine woodwork, and iguanas along seawalls and gardens. Protection here means a meticulous, low-profile program built around the property and its staff.

## The pests Estate Section homes face

### No-See-Ums & Mosquitoes

Ocean and lake on either side, plus lush gardens, keep biting midges and mosquitoes heavy across loggias, terraces, and pool decks.

### Roof Rats

Grand ficus hedges and mature canopy give roof rats direct routes into attics, soffits, and outbuildings.

### Termites

Subterranean and drywood termites threaten historic framing, millwork, and fine architectural detail.

### Iguanas & Ghost Ants

Iguanas colonize seawalls and gardens, and ghost ants trail indoors through the humid season.
