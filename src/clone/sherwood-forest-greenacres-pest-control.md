## Why Sherwood Forest homes need coverage

Sherwood Forest is an established Greenacres community with community lakes and a mature tree canopy over well-kept yards. The water breeds mosquitoes and midges, and the trees give roof rats a route to attics — a classic South Florida combination.

## The pests Sherwood Forest homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Community lakes keep mosquitoes and midges breeding through the wet season, hitting lakefront homes hardest.

### Ghost Ants

Moist, mature landscaping fuels fast-spreading ghost-ant colonies that trail indoors in thin lines.

### Palmetto Bugs

American roaches move in from mulch and drains after rain, turning up in garages and lanais.

### Roof Rats & Termites

Mature trees put roof rats into attics; subterranean termites attack from the soil under slabs.
