## Stinging insects are a year-round issue in Delray Beach

Our frost-free climate means wasps, hornets, and bees nest and stay active all year, and they love exactly the places people spend time — eaves, soffits, door frames, patios, lanais, and screen enclosures. A nest at a doorway or on a play set isn’t just a nuisance; for anyone with a sting allergy it’s a genuine hazard.

Honey bees are a different situation. When a colony moves into a wall void, soffit, or chimney, it becomes a structural problem — the comb and honey draw other pests and can stain and damage the building — and Florida also has more defensive Africanized honey bees. A structural colony should never be sprayed or sealed in by a homeowner, which only drives thousands of bees into the living space.

Bugsy’s performs hive removal and treatment — not live bee relocation. If bees are streaming in and out of a wall or soffit, do not spray or seal the opening yourself; it traps the colony and pushes bees indoors. Call a professional. Florida’s Africanized bees make this especially important.
