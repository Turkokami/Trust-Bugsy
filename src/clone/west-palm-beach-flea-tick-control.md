## Why fleas and ticks are relentless in West Palm Beach

Our frost-free climate means fleas and ticks breed all twelve months, so West Palm Beach yards and homes can stay infested long after you’d expect them to fade. And the adults you see are only about 5% of the problem — the eggs, larvae, and pupae hidden in carpet, bedding, and shaded yard areas are the rest.

That’s why spot-treating pets or spraying only the adults never works for long. Effective control treats the whole life cycle, across both the yard where they’re picked up and the interior where they develop.
