Frenchman’s Reserve is a gated golf community built around lakes and lush, irrigated landscaping. Those water features and grounds keep mosquitoes, midges, and ants active nearly year-round, and lakefront and fairway homes feel it first.

## The pests Frenchman’s Reserve homes face

### Mosquitoes & No-See-Ums

Golf-course lakes and water features are prime breeding grounds; mosquitoes and midges hit lake- and fairway-front homes hardest at dawn and dusk.

### Roof Rats

Mature, manicured landscaping and canopy give roof rats a direct route into attics, soffits, and outdoor kitchens.

### Ghost & White-Footed Ants

Lush, irrigated grounds feed big multi-queen ant colonies that trail indoors along counters and baseboards.

### Termites & Iguanas

Subterranean termites attack from moist soil; iguanas colonize lake banks and seawalls, damaging landscaping.
