A chinch bug infestation can kill large sections of St. Augustine grass in just days. The problem is that early chinch bug damage looks like drought stress, and homeowners often spend weeks watering a lawn that’s actually being destroyed by insects. ## What Are Chinch Bugs?

Southern chinch bugs (Blissus insularis) are tiny insects — adults are only 1/6 to 1/5 inch long — with black bodies and white wings folded flat across the back. Nymphs are bright red-orange with a white band across the body, turning darker as they mature. They’re extremely active in hot, dry, sunny conditions and populations can reach 20–30 bugs per square foot in severe infestations.

## How to Identify Chinch Bug Damage

- Irregular yellow or brown patches in the lawn, spreading outward from the edges — especially in the hottest, sunniest areas
- Grass that doesn’t recover after watering (unlike true drought stress)
- Damage typically starts near sidewalks, driveways, and walls that retain heat
- Parting the grass at the edge of a damaged area reveals the tiny insects running away from light

## The Soap Flush Test

To confirm chinch bugs: mix 2 tablespoons of dish soap in 1 gallon of water and pour it slowly over a 2-square-foot area at the edge of suspected damage. Wait 2–3 minutes and watch for tiny insects rising to the surface. More than 20 chinch bugs per square foot confirms an active infestation requiring professional treatment.

## Why Chinch Bugs Are Worse in South Florida

South Florida’s heat, drought conditions in the dry season, and the dominance of St. Augustine grass — their preferred food plant — make ![Red and black boxelder bugs clustered on a plant stem](https://trustbugsy.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Gemini_Generated_Image_s0xx24s0xx24s0xx.webp) Palm Beach and Broward counties among the highest-chinch-bug-pressure areas in the country. Thick, water-stressed St. Augustine in sunny areas is the #1 target.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can chinch bug damage be reversed?
If caught early, yes — the grass crown and root system may still be intact and the lawn can recover with treatment and proper watering. Damage that has been ongoing for 2+ weeks may have killed the grass down to the root, requiring resodding.

### Will watering more help chinch bug damage?
No — chinch bug damage is often mistaken for drought stress and homeowners over-water trying to fix it. The brown patches are caused by the bugs’ toxic saliva, not water deficiency. Excess irrigation can actually increase chinch bug populations by weakening grass.

### Are chinch bugs resistant to pesticides?
Some populations have developed resistance to pyrethroid insecticides. Bugsy’s uses professional-grade insecticides with multiple modes of action to overcome resistance, and rotates chemistries between treatments to prevent further development.
